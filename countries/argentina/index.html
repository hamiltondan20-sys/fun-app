Argentina

Argentina is a strong fit for travelers who want a mix of city life, wine country, mountain scenery, and big open landscapes.

Best for
City breaks, food and wine, nature, and longer scenic trips.
What stands out
Buenos Aires brings the energy, while Mendoza, Patagonia, and the northwest give the trip very different moods.
Good to remember
Distances are large, so choose a region or build in real travel days instead of trying to cover the whole country.
