Australia

For travelers who want urban icons and real outdoor payoff, with city planning that leaves enough room for scenery to matter.

Australia works best when travelers accept that the major cities and beach or nature experiences each need their own rhythm.

Australia is often at its best when the traveler stops separating the city from the outdoors. Coastal walks, harbor views, beaches, and the broader pace of the place all shape the trip just as much as museums or restaurant reservations do.

That means the itinerary usually gets better when it gives those elements time instead of trying to fill every day like a dense urban checklist.

Best for
Harbor cities, coastal escapes, outdoors-focused urban trips, and longer-haul vacations worth stretching out.
What stands out
Scenery is often central to the trip, and city days usually feel strongest when they leave time for the outdoors.
Good to remember
Trying to do too much in one day can flatten the very scenery and atmosphere that make the trip worth taking.

Where to go in Australia

  • CairnsBest for the Great Barrier Reef, rainforest, tropical weather, and a compact outdoor-focused base.
  • Great Barrier ReefBest for snorkeling, diving, reef wildlife, and tropical trips built around the water.
  • Great Ocean RoadBest for coastal drives, cliff views, rainforest stops, and a road trip that earns a slower pace.
  • K'gariBest for 4WD beach roads, freshwater lakes, dunes, and an adventurous Australian island escape.
  • MelbourneBest for coffee, art, neighborhoods, sports, and a city trip that rewards wandering beyond the main sights.
  • Surfers ParadiseBest for easy beach holidays, surf lessons, family attractions, and a Gold Coast trip with plenty of options.
  • SydneyBest for harbor views, beaches, coastal walking, and city trips that mix landmarks with outdoors time.
  • TasmaniaBest for wild coastlines, food, hiking, and a compact island road trip with a strong local identity.
  • UluruBest for desert landscapes, Indigenous culture, sunrise color, and a focused outback stay.
