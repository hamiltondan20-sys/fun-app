Rwanda

Rwanda is a thoughtful choice for travelers who want wildlife, green hills, clean cities, and a compact East African route.

Best for
Gorilla trekking, nature, culture, and carefully planned family adventures.
What stands out
Kigali, Volcanoes National Park, and the lake country offer a strong city-to-nature rhythm.
Good to remember
Trekking permits, fitness, weather, and transfer time should shape the plan from the beginning.
