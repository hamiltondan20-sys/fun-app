South Africa

South Africa is a flexible destination for travelers who want food, wine, coastlines, wildlife, city energy, and a serious sense of landscape.

Best for
Road trips, safari, food and wine, couples, and adventure.
What stands out
Cape Town, the Winelands, Johannesburg, Durban, and Kruger each play a different role.
Good to remember
Pick a clear route and do not underestimate the distance between the experiences that look close together.
