Colombia

Colombia is a lively choice for travelers who want colorful cities, warm-weather escapes, coffee country, and strong food and music scenes.

Best for
City energy, beaches, coffee, culture, and value-minded adventures.
What stands out
Bogota, Medellin, Cartagena, and the coffee region all bring a different pace.
Good to remember
Plan around altitude, heat, and transfer time so the trip has room to settle in between regions.
