Russia

Russia offers major historic cities, museums, architecture, and a very different scale of cultural travel.

Best for
Museums, architecture, history, and travelers seeking a deep urban experience.
What stands out
Moscow and Saint Petersburg each need their own focused plan.
Good to remember
Check current entry, safety, payment, and transport requirements before treating this as a live trip option.

Where to go in Russia

  • AltaiBest for mountains, rivers, remote roads, and nature trips that feel far from conventional resort travel.
  • KamchatkaBest for volcanoes, hot springs, wildlife, and remote adventure for travelers comfortable with active days.
  • Lake BaikalBest for vast lake scenery, winter landscapes, village stays, and quiet nature-focused travel.
  • Lena PillarsBest for unusual geology, river travel, and remote landscape trips where the journey is part of the story.
  • MoscowBest for grand public spaces, art, history, and a city break with strong contrasts between old and modern.
  • Saint PetersburgBest for grand architecture, museums, canals, and a slower cultural city break with long summer evenings.
  • Solovetsky IslandsBest for remote history, stark coastal scenery, and travelers drawn to quiet, reflective places.
