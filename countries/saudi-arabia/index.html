Saudi Arabia

Saudi Arabia is an emerging destination for travelers who want striking landscapes, history, modern cities, and a trip that feels genuinely new.

Best for
Culture, desert scenery, architecture, road trips, and travelers seeking a different kind of city break.
What stands out
Riyadh, Jeddah, AlUla, and the Red Sea each create a different reason to visit.
Good to remember
Local customs, weather, and changing access rules should be checked close to the trip.

Where to go in Saudi Arabia

  • AlUlaDesert landscapes, monumental sites, and travel days that feel strongest when the scenery is given real time.
  • JeddahRed Sea air, old-town contrast, and city days that work best when waterfront time and heritage both have room.
  • RiyadhModern scale, stronger evening rhythm, and city days that feel best when the plan respects distance and heat.
