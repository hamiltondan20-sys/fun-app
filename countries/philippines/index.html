Philippines

The Philippines is a good fit for travelers who want beaches, islands, warm hospitality, city energy, and plenty of active add-ons.

Best for
Island trips, diving, beaches, value, and adventurous couples vacations.
What stands out
Manila, Cebu, Palawan, Boracay, Bohol, and Siargao all call for different route choices.
Good to remember
Domestic flights and boat schedules take planning, so limit the number of islands in a single trip.
