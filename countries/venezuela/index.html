Venezuela

Venezuela has extraordinary landscapes, from Caribbean coastlines to table-top mountains, for travelers seeking a more unusual route.

Best for
Nature, adventure, landscapes, and experienced travelers.
What stands out
Caracas, the coast, Angel Falls, and Los Roques each require different logistics.
Good to remember
Current safety, access, transport, and trusted local support need to be confirmed before planning.

Where to go in Venezuela

  • Angel FallsBest for remote adventure, river journeys, and travelers who want the destination itself to feel hard-earned.
