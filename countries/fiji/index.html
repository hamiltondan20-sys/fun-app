Fiji

Fiji works well for travelers who want friendly island time, clear water, soft adventure, and a tropical trip that can suit families or couples.

Best for
Beach trips, families, snorkeling, couples, and easygoing island travel.
What stands out
Nadi is the practical gateway, while the Coral Coast, Denarau, and outer islands offer different levels of seclusion.
Good to remember
Build in transfer and weather flexibility, especially when the trip includes more than one island.
