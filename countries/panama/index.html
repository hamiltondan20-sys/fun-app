Panama

Panama works well for travelers who want a modern city, tropical islands, rainforest, and an easy Central American mix.

Best for
Warm-weather escapes, nature, beaches, food, and short multi-stop trips.
What stands out
Panama City, the Canal, Bocas del Toro, and the Pacific coast offer different trip styles.
Good to remember
Choose coast and city priorities early because transfers can make a short trip feel busy.

Where to go in Panama

  • Panama CanalBest for engineering, tropical city breaks, canal watching, and a compact itinerary that can add islands or rainforest.
