Switzerland

Switzerland works for travelers who want beautiful cities, mountain views, efficient trains, and scenery that is easy to build into the route.

Best for
Scenic rail, mountains, couples, family trips, and polished city breaks.
What stands out
Zurich, Lucerne, Geneva, Interlaken, and Zermatt each suit a different version of the trip.
Good to remember
Choose the scenic experiences first, then use rail connections to keep the route realistic.
