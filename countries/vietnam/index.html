Vietnam

Vietnam is a strong value destination for travelers who want excellent food, lively cities, history, coastlines, and flexible route options.

Best for
Food, culture, adventure, value, and multi-city trips.
What stands out
Hanoi, Hoi An, Da Nang, Hue, Ho Chi Minh City, and the bays each offer a distinct rhythm.
Good to remember
Build around the region and season rather than trying to see the entire country on a short trip.

Where to go in Vietnam

  • Da LatCooler air, hillside cafes, and slower highland days that work best when they stay relaxed.
  • Da NangBest for beach time, easy food, mountain views, and a practical central Vietnam base with Hoi An nearby.
  • Ha Long BayBest for limestone islands, boat stays, seafood, and a scenic reset from Hanoi.
  • HaiphongHarbor-city energy, local food, and an easier urban pace that feels strongest when the day stays local.
  • HanoiBest for street food, old streets, lakes, history, and a city break that feels vivid from breakfast through late...
  • Ho Chi Minh CityBest for food, nightlife, history, coffee, and a high-energy city break with easy day-trip options.
  • Hoi AnBest for lantern-lit evenings, tailoring, food, beaches, and a slower central Vietnam stay.
  • HueBest for imperial history, river views, thoughtful food, and a quieter cultural stop between Hanoi and Hoi An.
  • Nha TrangBeach time, coastal light, and resort-city days that feel best when one mood carries the plan.
  • Phong Nha CavesBest for caves, river scenery, active adventure, and a quieter alternative to Vietnam's busier routes.
  • Phu QuocBest for beach recovery, snorkeling, seafood, and a tropical finish to a Vietnam itinerary.
  • SapaMountain scenery, village views, and travel days that work best when the pace stays gentle.
