Croatia

Croatia works especially well for travelers who want clear water, old towns, island time, and a Mediterranean trip with strong visual payoff.

Best for
Beach trips, couples, sailing, history, and scenic road trips.
What stands out
Dubrovnik, Split, Hvar, and the Istrian coast offer different versions of the country.
Good to remember
Ferry schedules and summer crowds shape the route, so protect the key island or coastal days early.
