French Polynesia

French Polynesia is a strong match for travelers who want lagoons, mountain backdrops, soft adventure, and slower island time.

Best for
Honeymoons, luxury, snorkeling, romance, and restorative beach trips.
What stands out
Tahiti, Moorea, and Bora Bora can each play a different role in the trip.
Good to remember
Island transfers and resort location matter, so choose the rhythm before adding more islands.
