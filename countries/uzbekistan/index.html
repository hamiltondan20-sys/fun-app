Uzbekistan

Uzbekistan suits travelers who want Silk Road history, striking architecture, markets, and an itinerary that feels genuinely different.

Best for
History, architecture, food, photography, and adventurous city travel.
What stands out
Tashkent, Samarkand, Bukhara, and Khiva create a strong historic route.
Good to remember
Rail and transfer days matter, so keep the route focused and allow time between cities.

Where to go in Uzbekistan

  • BukharaMadrasah courtyards, old-city calm, and heritage days that work best when the route stays compact and unhurried.
  • KhivaWalled-city atmosphere, slower walking, and historic days that feel strongest when the whole old town becomes the plan.
  • SamarkandBest for Silk Road architecture, grand blue-tiled monuments, markets, and a memorable history-led city stay.
  • TashkentBroad boulevards, market energy, and city days that feel strongest when modern ease and old-city texture stay balanced.
