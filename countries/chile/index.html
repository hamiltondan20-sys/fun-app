Chile

Chile works well for travelers who want dramatic scenery, outdoor adventure, and a city base with easy access to very different landscapes.

Best for
Nature, road trips, hiking, wine, and travelers who like a clear sense of place.
What stands out
Santiago, the Atacama, the lake district, and Patagonia each feel like a different trip.
Good to remember
Pick one geographic section for a shorter vacation and protect transfer time between regions.
