Iran

Iran offers extraordinary architecture, food, gardens, and historic cities for travelers seeking a deeply cultural experience.

Best for
History, architecture, food, photography, and experienced cultural travelers.
What stands out
Tehran, Isfahan, Shiraz, and Yazd create a rich first route.
Good to remember
Visa, entry, payment, dress, internet, and current safety requirements must be checked carefully.

Where to go in Iran

  • IsfahanBest for grand squares, bridges, tiled architecture, and a slower cultural city break.
