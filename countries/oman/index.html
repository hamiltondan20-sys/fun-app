Oman

Oman is a strong choice for travelers who want dramatic coastlines, desert landscapes, mountain drives, and a quieter Gulf experience.

Best for
Road trips, nature, beaches, culture, and adventurous travelers.
What stands out
Muscat, the wadis, Wahiba Sands, Nizwa, and Salalah each support a different route.
Good to remember
A car and realistic driving days make the destination much easier to enjoy.

Where to go in Oman

  • MuscatSea-and-mountain calm, lower-rise city beauty, and days that feel best when the schedule stays clear and scenic.
