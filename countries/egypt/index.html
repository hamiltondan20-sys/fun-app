Egypt

Egypt is a high-impact choice for travelers who want ancient history, warm-weather escapes, river scenery, and experiences that feel unlike anywhere else.

Best for
History, family trips, river cruises, warm weather, and first-time cultural travel.
What stands out
Cairo, Luxor, Aswan, the Red Sea, and Alexandria create very different trip shapes.
Good to remember
Heat, transfer time, and guided access matter, so protect the headline sites without overloading the day.

Where to go in Egypt

  • AlexandriaMediterranean air, literary texture, and city days that feel strongest when the waterfront and old-city character stay...
  • AswanBest for Nile scenery, temples, felucca rides, and a calmer southern Egypt experience.
  • CairoBest for ancient history, museums, river views, and a city that rewards guided context and realistic pacing.
  • Egyptian Pyramids and Valley of the KingsBest for ancient history, river travel, and a trip that pairs Cairo's scale with Luxor's open-air archaeology.
  • HurghadaResort beaches, easy boat outings, and Red Sea travel that works best when one clear mood carries the day.
  • LuxorBest for temples, tombs, river views, and a concentrated history trip with an unforgettable sense of place.
  • Sharm El SheikhBest for Red Sea snorkeling, resort recovery, desert add-ons, and warm-weather beach time.
