Country guide

United States

For travelers who want range, from major urban icons to entertainment and nature pairings, with planning that stays local instead of overly broad.

Trips in the United States usually feel better when each destination is treated on its own terms, because city scale, transport, and pace vary widely.

The United States is not one travel rhythm. That is the most important thing to understand before choosing a city or building an itinerary here. New York, Los Angeles, Orlando, and San Francisco all behave differently enough that broad assumptions tend to weaken the trip.

The country works best when each destination is treated on its own terms and daily movement is planned with common sense. Once that happens, the variety becomes an advantage instead of a complication.

Best for
Big-city highlights, road-trip add-ons, entertainment, theme parks, and national-park pairings.
What stands out
Destinations can be very different from each other, so local planning matters more than broad country assumptions.
Good to remember
Distance and traffic can reshape a day quickly, so grouping stops tightly matters.
