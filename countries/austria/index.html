Austria

Austria suits travelers who want graceful cities, music, cafes, alpine scenery, and easy rail-connected add-ons.

Best for
City breaks, culture, winter trips, romance, and scenic rail travel.
What stands out
Vienna brings the museums and cafes, while Salzburg, Innsbruck, and the lakes add scenery.
Good to remember
A short trip can work beautifully with one city and one nearby escape instead of constant hotel changes.
