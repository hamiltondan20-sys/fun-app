Italy

For travelers who want history, food, and iconic urban experiences without losing the slower pleasures that make Italy feel like Italy.

Italy usually feels strongest when the trip protects time for the essentials but still leaves space for wandering, meals, and slower evenings.

Italy is one of the easiest countries to overbuild because the sights are so obvious and so tempting to stack. The stronger version usually accepts that piazzas, longer meals, and slower evenings are not interruptions to the trip. They are part of the destination’s real payoff.

Whether the traveler starts in Rome or another major city, the trip generally improves once the essentials are protected and the rest of the day is allowed to stay a little softer.

Best for
History, iconic sights, food, romance, and repeat-worthy city breaks.
What stands out
Big landmarks matter, but so do piazzas, neighborhood walks, and long meals.
Good to remember
Transit, crowds, and old-city walking can make overpacked days feel heavier than they look on paper.
