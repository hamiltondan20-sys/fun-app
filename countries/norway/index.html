Norway

Norway is a natural choice for travelers who want fjords, clean cities, outdoor adventure, and scenery that shapes the whole route.

Best for
Nature, hiking, scenic rail, winter travel, and adventurous couples trips.
What stands out
Oslo, Bergen, Tromso, and the fjord country each work best with a different trip pace.
Good to remember
Let the scenery set the route and avoid adding too many long transfers to a short visit.
