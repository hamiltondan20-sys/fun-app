Ireland

Ireland works well for travelers who want welcoming cities, dramatic coastlines, live music, and road trips with a strong sense of place.

Best for
Road trips, couples, pubs and music, scenery, and relaxed city breaks.
What stands out
Dublin is easy to start with, while Galway, Cork, Killarney, and the west coast add more atmosphere.
Good to remember
Driving times are often slower than the map suggests, so leave room for stops and weather.
