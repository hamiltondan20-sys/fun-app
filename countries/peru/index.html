Peru

Peru gives travelers a memorable mix of ancient history, mountain scenery, food, and high-impact cultural experiences.

Best for
History, food, hiking, culture, and once-in-a-lifetime highlights.
What stands out
Lima, Cusco, the Sacred Valley, and Machu Picchu can create a strong first route.
Good to remember
Altitude changes the pace, so leave room to adjust before the biggest hikes or sightseeing days.
