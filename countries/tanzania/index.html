Tanzania

Tanzania is a strong match for travelers who want wildlife, mountain scenery, and an easy way to add beach time to a major adventure.

Best for
Safari, beaches, hiking, honeymoon trips, and once-in-a-lifetime travel.
What stands out
Serengeti, Ngorongoro, Kilimanjaro, Arusha, and Zanzibar support different trip priorities.
Good to remember
Use the safari and flight logistics to anchor the plan, then add beach time without rushing the handoff.
