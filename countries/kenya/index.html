Kenya

Kenya combines lively cities, coastal downtime, and some of the world's most memorable wildlife experiences.

Best for
Safari, family adventures, beach extensions, photography, and nature-led travel.
What stands out
Nairobi, Maasai Mara, Mombasa, and Diani Beach can form a city-to-wildlife-to-coast route.
Good to remember
Transfers and park schedules shape the itinerary, so use realistic travel days and trusted operators.
