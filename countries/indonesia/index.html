Indonesia

Indonesia suits travelers who want tropical scenery, strong local culture, beaches, food, and a trip that can shift from restful to adventurous.

Best for
Beach trips, wellness, couples, surfing, culture, and island-hopping.
What stands out
Bali and Ubud are easy starting points, while Java, Lombok, and Komodo bring a more active feel.
Good to remember
Island transfers take real time, so fewer bases usually create a better vacation.

Where to go in Indonesia

  • BaliBest for beach time, wellness, temples, rice fields, and a trip that can be as restful or adventurous as the traveler...
  • BorobudurBest for monumental Buddhist heritage, sunrise scenery, and a calm cultural stop paired with Central Java towns.
  • Gili AirSmall-island calm, beach time, and travel days that feel strongest when the plan stays intentionally light.
  • JakartaBest for food, contemporary culture, shopping, and travelers who want to see Indonesia through its energetic capital.
  • KomodoBoat days, dramatic scenery, and adventure-led travel that works best when one outing defines the day.
  • LombokBest for quieter beaches, waterfalls, surf, and a more adventurous island alternative to Bali.
  • Raja AmpatRemote-water scenery, boat time, and nature-led days that work best when the schedule stays minimal.
  • SurabayaUrban energy, food, and a city rhythm that feels best when the day stays focused and practical.
  • UbudBest for rice terraces, temples, wellness, art, and travelers who want Bali to feel calmer and more grounded.
  • YogyakartaBest for temples, batik, food, and a culturally rich Java city break with strong day-trip options.
