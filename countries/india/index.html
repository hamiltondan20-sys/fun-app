India

India is a huge, high-energy destination with city life, food, history, spirituality, beaches, and landscapes that demand a focused route.

Best for
Food, culture, history, family journeys, and immersive first-time travel.
What stands out
Delhi, Agra, Jaipur, Mumbai, Kerala, Goa, and the Himalayas all deserve different planning logic.
Good to remember
Start with one region and allow more recovery time than a map-based itinerary usually suggests.
