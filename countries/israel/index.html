Israel

Israel offers layered history, food, coastline, and highly contrasting city and landscape experiences.

Best for
History, food, beaches, faith-related travel, and culturally focused trips.
What stands out
Jerusalem, Tel Aviv, the Galilee, and the Dead Sea create different travel moods.
Good to remember
Current entry, safety, access, and local conditions must be checked close to travel.

Where to go in Israel

  • JerusalemBest for layered history, meaningful food, markets, and days where a small area can hold centuries of stories.
  • Tel AvivBeach-city ease, food, and urban days that work best when one neighborhood carries the energy from morning into night.
