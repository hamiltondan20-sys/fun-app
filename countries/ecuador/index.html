Ecuador

Ecuador is a compact destination with colonial cities, volcanoes, cloud forest, coast, and the Galapagos as a major add-on.

Best for
Nature, family trips, wildlife, hiking, and varied short routes.
What stands out
Quito, the Andes, the Amazon, the coast, and the Galapagos create very different experiences.
Good to remember
Altitude and flight logistics matter, so avoid making every day an active one.

Where to go in Ecuador

  • Ecuadorian AndesBest for volcano scenery, highland markets, compact road trips, and a strong mix of nature and culture.
  • Galapagos IslandsBest for wildlife, snorkeling, small-boat exploration, and a nature trip where flexibility matters more than volume.
