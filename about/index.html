How we review guides

About The Fullest Life Travel

How destination recommendations are checked, documented and reviewed before they become part of a guide.

How source reviews work

Our source-review process starts with the destination's official tourism board. We then check attractions and businesses against their own current websites, including the exact branch, access information and operating evidence. Where available, we add independent corroboration. Dated provenance records keep the place name, checked URLs and evidence together. A website review is not a personal visit or a guarantee that a venue will be open on your travel date.

What the records mean

Review status is documented in docs/city-verification-status.md, with per-place evidence in the source ledger and verification inventory. Source reviews are still in progress. Some published guides have passed automated content checks but have not completed a recorded source review. An automated match for a place name is not proof that the place exists or is currently open.

Why some destinations are missing

Pages that fail the publication checks are withheld, including pages with too few named places or generic filler. During source review, recommendations without reliable supporting evidence are left out rather than replaced with invented places. Publication checks and source verification are different: we do not claim that every published guide has been fully source-checked.

Suggest a correction

If a detail needs another look, send us the page, place name and a current official source where possible. Confirm opening times, access and booking details with the venue before traveling.
