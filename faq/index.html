Frequently asked questions

Answers about Horizon Bound trip planning, saved drafts, guide content, and travel information.

How does the planner work?

Start with your destination, dates, travelers, and trip style. Horizon Bound then turns those choices into a practical day-by-day starting point.

Can I change the plan?

Yes. Review the draft, adjust the details that matter, and generate a new version when the first pass does not feel right.

Are prices and availability live?

The planner does not promise live availability. Check the linked airline, hotel, or booking source before you commit.

Where does the guide information come from?

Guides are built from Horizon Bound's own destination research and updated as the catalogue grows.
