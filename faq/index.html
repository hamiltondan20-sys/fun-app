Frequently asked questions

Answers about The Fullest Life Travel trip planning, saved drafts, guide content, and travel information.

How does the planner work?

Start with your destination, dates, travelers, and trip style. We then turn those choices into a practical day-by-day starting point.

Can I change the plan?

Yes. Review the draft, adjust the details that matter, and generate a new version when the first pass does not feel right.

Are prices and availability live?

The planner does not promise live availability. Check the linked airline, hotel, or booking source before you commit.

Where does the guide information come from?

Guides are built from our own destination research and updated as the catalogue grows.

Help us improve trip planning?

Optional usage data helps us see which parts of the site need work. Your trip details are not included.
