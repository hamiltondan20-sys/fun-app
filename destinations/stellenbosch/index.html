Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Stellenbosch?

Spring, fall, and harvest season Stellenbosch is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Stellenbosch?

Stellenbosch town, Franschhoek, or a nearby practical base Use Stellenbosch town as the first base when possible, then add Franschhoek only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Stellenbosch?

Wine estates, a local food experience, and the trip's main day outing Protect Wine estates, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Stellenbosch feel like a trip.
