Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Hanoi?

Spring, fall, and cooler winter days Hanoi is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Hanoi?

Old Quarter, French Quarter, or West Lake Use Old Quarter as the first base when possible, then add French Quarter only if the extra move is worth the time.

How do I get around Hanoi?

Walking, rideshares, and short transfers Hanoi works best when food and neighborhood walking are treated as the main event, not filler between attractions.

What should I book in advance for Hanoi?

A food tour and any overnight bay trip Protect Old Quarter, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Hanoi feel like a trip.
