Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Valencia?

Spring, early summer, and fall Valencia is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Valencia?

Ciutat Vella, Ruzafa, or near the beach Use Ciutat Vella as the first base when possible, then add Ruzafa only if the extra move is worth the time.

How do I get around Valencia?

Walking, cycling, and transit Valencia shines when the itinerary mixes one cultural anchor with cycling, market time, and an unhurried meal.

What should I book in advance for Valencia?

City of Arts timing and a real paella lunch Protect City of Arts and Sciences, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Valencia feel like a trip.
