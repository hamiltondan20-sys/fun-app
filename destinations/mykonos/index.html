Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Mykonos?

Late spring through early fall Mykonos is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Mykonos?

Mykonos Town, Ornos, or a nearby practical base Use Mykonos Town as the first base when possible, then add Ornos only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Mykonos?

Mykonos Town, a local food experience, and the trip's main day outing Protect Mykonos Town, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Mykonos feel like a trip.
