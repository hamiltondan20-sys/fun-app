Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Cologne?

Spring, summer, and festive winter Cologne is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Cologne?

Altstadt, Belgisches Viertel, or a nearby practical base Use Altstadt as the first base when possible, then add Belgisches Viertel only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Cologne?

Cologne Cathedral, a local food experience, and the trip's main day outing Protect Cologne Cathedral, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Cologne feel like a trip.
