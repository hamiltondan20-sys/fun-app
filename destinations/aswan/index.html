Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Aswan?

Cooler months Aswan is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Aswan?

Nile Corniche, Elephantine Island, or a nearby practical base Use Nile Corniche as the first base when possible, then add Elephantine Island only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Aswan?

Philae Temple, a local food experience, and the trip's main day outing Protect Philae Temple, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Aswan feel like a trip.
