Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Jerusalem?

Old City or Mamilla Use Old City as the first base when possible, then add Mamilla only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Jerusalem?

Old City and Western Wall Protect Old City, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Jerusalem feel like a trip.

A first day in Jerusalem

Here is one way the planner could shape an opening day. Your version changes with your dates, pace, and who you are traveling with.

  1. 9:00 AM

    Start near Old City

    Use Old City to get oriented without asking the trip to cover too much ground.

  2. 11:00 AM

    Old City

    Give the first major highlight enough time to make Jerusalem feel real from the start.

  3. 1:30 PM

    Local lunch

    Make time for a market lunch with Middle Eastern specialties so the first meal becomes part of the destination instead of a blank gap.
