Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Tulum?

Dry season and shoulder months Tulum is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Tulum?

Tulum Pueblo, Beach Road, or a nearby practical base Use Tulum Pueblo as the first base when possible, then add Beach Road only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Tulum?

Tulum ruins, a local food experience, and the trip's main day outing Protect Tulum ruins, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Tulum feel like a trip.
