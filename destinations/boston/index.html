Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Boston?

Spring, summer, and fall Boston is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Boston?

Beacon Hill, Back Bay, or a nearby practical base Use Beacon Hill as the first base when possible, then add Back Bay only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Boston?

Freedom Trail, a local food experience, and the trip's main day outing Protect Freedom Trail, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Boston feel like a trip.
