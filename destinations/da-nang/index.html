Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Da Nang?

Dry season beach weather Da Nang is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Da Nang?

My Khe, Han River, or Son Tra Use My Khe as the first base when possible, then add Han River only if the extra move is worth the time.

How do I get around Da Nang?

Rideshares, walking, and day-trip drivers Da Nang works well when the trip keeps mornings for sights and afternoons for the beach, food, or a slower nearby town.

What should I book in advance for Da Nang?

Hoi An transfer, Ba Na Hills if desired, and local seafood Protect My Khe Beach, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Da Nang feel like a trip.
