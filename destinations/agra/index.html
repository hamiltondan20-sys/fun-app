Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Agra?

Cooler months Agra is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Agra?

Taj East Gate, Agra Fort, or a nearby practical base Use Taj East Gate as the first base when possible, then add Agra Fort only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Agra?

Taj Mahal, a local food experience, and the trip's main day outing Protect Taj Mahal, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Agra feel like a trip.
