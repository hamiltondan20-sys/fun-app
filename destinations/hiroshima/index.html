Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Hiroshima?

Spring and fall Hiroshima is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Hiroshima?

Near Peace Park, Hondori, or one night on Miyajima Use Peace Park as the first base when possible, then add Hondori only if the extra move is worth the time.

How do I get around Hiroshima?

Streetcar, ferry, and walking Give Hiroshima enough emotional and practical breathing room; it is better experienced as a focused two- or three-day stay than a rushed stop.

What should I book in advance for Hiroshima?

Miyajima timing and a local okonomiyaki table Protect Peace Memorial Park, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Hiroshima feel like a trip.
