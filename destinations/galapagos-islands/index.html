Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Galapagos Islands?

Santa Cruz or Isabela Use Santa Cruz as the first base when possible, then add Isabela only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Galapagos Islands?

Santa Cruz highlands and Tortuga Bay Protect Santa Cruz highlands, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Galapagos Islands feel like a trip.
