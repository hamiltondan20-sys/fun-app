Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Verona?

Spring, fall, and opera season Verona is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Verona?

Centro Storico, San Zeno, or a nearby practical base Use Centro Storico as the first base when possible, then add San Zeno only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Verona?

Arena di Verona, a local food experience, and the trip's main day outing Protect Arena di Verona, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Verona feel like a trip.
