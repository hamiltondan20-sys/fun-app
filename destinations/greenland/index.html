Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Greenland?

Ilulissat or Nuuk Use Ilulissat as the first base when possible, then add Nuuk only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Greenland?

Ilulissat Icefjord and Nuuk Protect Ilulissat Icefjord, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Greenland feel like a trip.
