Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Chicago?

Late spring through early fall Chicago is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Chicago?

The Loop, River North, or a nearby practical base Use The Loop as the first base when possible, then add River North only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Chicago?

Architecture river cruise, a local food experience, and the trip's main day outing Protect Architecture river cruise, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Chicago feel like a trip.
