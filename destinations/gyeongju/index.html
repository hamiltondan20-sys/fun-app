Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Gyeongju?

Spring and fall Gyeongju is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Gyeongju?

Historic Center, Bulguksa, or a nearby practical base Use Historic Center as the first base when possible, then add Bulguksa only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Gyeongju?

Bulguksa Temple, a local food experience, and the trip's main day outing Protect Bulguksa Temple, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Gyeongju feel like a trip.
