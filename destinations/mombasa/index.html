Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Mombasa?

Dry seasons and warm beach months Mombasa is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Mombasa?

Old Town, Nyali, or a nearby practical base Use Old Town as the first base when possible, then add Nyali only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Mombasa?

Fort Jesus, a local food experience, and the trip's main day outing Protect Fort Jesus, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Mombasa feel like a trip.
