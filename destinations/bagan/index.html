Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Bagan and Mandalay?

Old Bagan or Mandalay Use Old Bagan as the first base when possible, then add Mandalay only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Bagan and Mandalay?

Bagan temples and Mandalay Hill Protect Bagan temples, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Bagan and Mandalay feel like a trip.
