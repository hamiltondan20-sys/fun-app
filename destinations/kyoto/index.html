Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Kyoto?

Gion or Higashiyama Use Gion as the first base when possible, then add Higashiyama only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Kyoto?

Fushimi Inari and Kiyomizu-dera Protect Fushimi Inari, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Kyoto feel like a trip.

A first day in Kyoto

Here is one way the planner could shape an opening day. Your version changes with your dates, pace, and who you are traveling with.

  1. 9:00 AM

    Start near Gion

    Use Gion to get oriented without asking the trip to cover too much ground.

  2. 11:00 AM

    Fushimi Inari

    Give the first major highlight enough time to make Kyoto feel real from the start.

  3. 1:30 PM

    Local lunch

    Make time for a Kyoto set meal or Nishiki Market stop so the first meal becomes part of the destination instead of a blank gap.
