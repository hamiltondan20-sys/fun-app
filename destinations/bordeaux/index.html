Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Bordeaux?

Spring, summer, and fall Bordeaux is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Bordeaux?

Golden Triangle, Chartrons, or a nearby practical base Use Golden Triangle as the first base when possible, then add Chartrons only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Bordeaux?

Place de la Bourse, a local food experience, and the trip's main day outing Protect Place de la Bourse, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Bordeaux feel like a trip.
