Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Bali?

Dry season and shoulder months Bali is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Bali?

Ubud plus one southern coast base Use Ubud as the first base when possible, then add Seminyak only if the extra move is worth the time.

How do I get around Bali?

Driver days, rideshares, and short walks Bali feels much better when the trip picks two bases instead of trying to cross the island every day.

What should I book in advance for Bali?

Airport transfer, beach clubs, and standout dinners Protect Uluwatu Temple, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Bali feel like a trip.
