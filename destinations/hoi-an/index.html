Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Hoi An?

Dry season and shoulder months Hoi An is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Hoi An?

Old Town, Cam Pho, or a nearby practical base Use Old Town as the first base when possible, then add Cam Pho only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Hoi An?

Old Town, a local food experience, and the trip's main day outing Protect Old Town, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Hoi An feel like a trip.
