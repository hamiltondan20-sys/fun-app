Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Ecuadorian Andes?

Quito or Cotopaxi corridor Use Quito as the first base when possible, then add Cotopaxi corridor only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Ecuadorian Andes?

Cotopaxi and Quilotoa Protect Cotopaxi, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Ecuadorian Andes feel like a trip.

  1. 9:00 AM

  2. 11:00 AM
