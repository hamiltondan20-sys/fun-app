Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Milan?

Spring, fall, and design season Milan is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Milan?

Brera, Centro Storico, or Navigli Use Brera as the first base when possible, then add Duomo only if the extra move is worth the time.

How do I get around Milan?

Metro, tram, and walking Milan works best when the trip blends the headline sights with aperitivo and one nearby neighborhood rather than chasing every district.

What should I book in advance for Milan?

The Last Supper, rooftop Duomo time, and lake trains Protect Duomo, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Milan feel like a trip.
