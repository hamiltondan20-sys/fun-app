Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Iceland?

Reykjavik or South Coast Use Reykjavik as the first base when possible, then add South Coast only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Iceland?

Golden Circle and South Coast Protect Golden Circle, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Iceland feel like a trip.

A first day in Iceland

Here is one way the planner could shape an opening day. Your version changes with your dates, pace, and who you are traveling with.

  1. 9:00 AM

    Start near Reykjavik

    Use Reykjavik to get oriented without asking the trip to cover too much ground.

  2. 11:00 AM

    Golden Circle

    Give the first major highlight enough time to make Iceland feel real from the start.

  3. 1:30 PM

    Local lunch

    Make time for a Reykjavik seafood or bakery stop so the first meal becomes part of the destination instead of a blank gap.
