Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Nairobi?

Dry seasons and flexible shoulder months Nairobi is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Nairobi?

Karen, Westlands, or near the airport Use Karen as the first base when possible, then add Westlands only if the extra move is worth the time.

How do I get around Nairobi?

Rideshares and trusted drivers Nairobi works well when the first day is lighter after the flight, then the city and wildlife stops are grouped by area.

What should I book in advance for Nairobi?

Park entry, wildlife experiences, and safari connections Protect Nairobi National Park, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Nairobi feel like a trip.
