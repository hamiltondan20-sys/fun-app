Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Nice?

Late spring through early fall Nice is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Nice?

Old Nice, Carré d'Or, or near the Promenade Use Old Nice as the first base when possible, then add Promenade only if the extra move is worth the time.

How do I get around Nice?

Walk, tram, and short regional train trips Nice works best as a base for one coastal outing at a time, with enough room for a long lunch and beach or market time.

What should I book in advance for Nice?

Villa Ephrussi, coastal day trips, and standout dinners Protect Promenade des Anglais, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Nice feel like a trip.
