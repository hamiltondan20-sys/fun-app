Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Santorini?

Oia or Fira Use Oia as the first base when possible, then add Fira only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Santorini?

Oia and Fira caldera Protect Oia, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Santorini feel like a trip.

A first day in Santorini

Here is one way the planner could shape an opening day. Your version changes with your dates, pace, and who you are traveling with.

  1. 9:00 AM

    Start near Oia

    Use Oia to get oriented without asking the trip to cover too much ground.

  2. 11:00 AM

    Oia

    Give the first major highlight enough time to make Santorini feel real from the start.

  3. 1:30 PM

    Local lunch

    Make time for a cliffside Greek dinner so the first meal becomes part of the destination instead of a blank gap.
