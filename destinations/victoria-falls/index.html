Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Victoria Falls?

Falls area or Victoria Falls town Use Falls area as the first base when possible, then add Victoria Falls town only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Victoria Falls?

Main Falls and Zambezi River Protect Main Falls, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Victoria Falls feel like a trip.

A first day in Victoria Falls

Here is one way the planner could shape an opening day. Your version changes with your dates, pace, and who you are traveling with.

  1. 9:00 AM

    Start near Falls area

    Use Falls area to get oriented without asking the trip to cover too much ground.

  2. 11:00 AM

    Main Falls

    Give the first major highlight enough time to make Victoria Falls feel real from the start.

  3. 1:30 PM

    Local lunch

    Make time for a lodge dinner or riverside meal so the first meal becomes part of the destination instead of a blank gap.
