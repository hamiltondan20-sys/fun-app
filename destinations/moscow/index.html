A first day in Moscow

Here is one way the planner could shape an opening day. Your version changes with your dates, pace, and who you are traveling with.

  1. 9:00 AM

    Start near Red Square

    Use Red Square to get oriented without asking the trip to cover too much ground.

  2. 11:00 AM

    Red Square

    Give the first major highlight enough time to make Moscow feel real from the start.

  3. 1:30 PM

    Local lunch

    Make time for a Russian restaurant or market stop so the first meal becomes part of the destination instead of a blank gap.
