Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Moscow?

Red Square or Arbat Use Red Square as the first base when possible, then add Arbat only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Moscow?

Red Square and Kremlin Protect Red Square, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Moscow feel like a trip.

A first day in Moscow

Here is one way the planner could shape an opening day. Your version changes with your dates, pace, and who you are traveling with.

  1. 9:00 AM

    Start near Red Square

    Use Red Square to get oriented without asking the trip to cover too much ground.

  2. 11:00 AM

    Red Square

    Give the first major highlight enough time to make Moscow feel real from the start.

  3. 1:30 PM

    Local lunch

    Make time for a Russian restaurant or market stop so the first meal becomes part of the destination instead of a blank gap.
