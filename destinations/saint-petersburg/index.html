Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Saint Petersburg?

Nevsky Prospect or Palace Square Use Nevsky Prospect as the first base when possible, then add Palace Square only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Saint Petersburg?

Hermitage Museum and Church of the Savior on Spilled Blood Protect Hermitage Museum, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Saint Petersburg feel like a trip.

A first day in Saint Petersburg

Here is one way the planner could shape an opening day. Your version changes with your dates, pace, and who you are traveling with.

  1. 9:00 AM

    Start near Nevsky Prospect

    Use Nevsky Prospect to get oriented without asking the trip to cover too much ground.

  2. 11:00 AM

    Hermitage Museum

    Give the first major highlight enough time to make Saint Petersburg feel real from the start.

  3. 1:30 PM

    Local lunch

    Make time for a Russian comfort-food restaurant or bakery stop so the first meal becomes part of the destination instead of a blank gap.
