A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Dead Sea?

Dead Sea resorts or Madaba Use Dead Sea resorts as the first base when possible, then add Madaba only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Dead Sea?

Dead Sea shore and Mount Nebo Protect Dead Sea shore, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Dead Sea feel like a trip.
