Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Cappadocia?

Goreme or Uchisar Use Goreme as the first base when possible, then add Uchisar only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Cappadocia?

Goreme and Hot-air balloon flight Protect Goreme, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Cappadocia feel like a trip.
