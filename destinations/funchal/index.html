Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Funchal?

Spring, fall, and mild winter Funchal is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Funchal?

Old Town, Lido, or a nearby practical base Use Old Town as the first base when possible, then add Lido only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Funchal?

Funchal Old Town, a local food experience, and the trip's main day outing Protect Funchal Old Town, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Funchal feel like a trip.
