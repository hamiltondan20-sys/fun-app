Los Angeles

For travelers who want the city’s major icons, but also need help turning a sprawling destination into days that actually make sense.

Best for
First-time California trips, entertainment fans, beach-city combinations, and travelers who like neighborhoods with totally different moods.
Ideal trip length
4 to 5 days if users want to split the city into a few distinct area-based days.
How to plan it
Los Angeles gets much better when each day stays on one side of town and the itinerary treats driving time as a real part of the plan.

Los Angeles is not a city that rewards overconfidence. It is huge, slow-moving in all the ways people warn you about, and full of neighborhoods that feel more like separate cities than connected districts. That is exactly why some LA trips feel disjointed while others feel effortless.

The best Los Angeles trips usually commit to one part of town at a time. A beach day, a studio-and-Hollywood day, or an arts-and-food day will almost always feel better than trying to pull Santa Monica, Griffith, Beverly Hills, and downtown into the same itinerary.
