Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Seville?

Spring and fall Seville is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Seville?

Santa Cruz, El Arenal, or Alameda Use Santa Cruz as the first base when possible, then add Triana only if the extra move is worth the time.

How do I get around Seville?

Walking and short taxi rides Seville is built around late meals and evening life, so do not schedule the day as if the city runs on an early timetable.

What should I book in advance for Seville?

Alcazar, flamenco, and a special tapas evening Protect Real Alcazar, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Seville feel like a trip.
