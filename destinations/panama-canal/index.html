Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Panama Canal?

Miraflores or Casco Viejo Use Miraflores as the first base when possible, then add Casco Viejo only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Panama Canal?

Miraflores Locks and Casco Viejo Protect Miraflores Locks, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Panama Canal feel like a trip.

A first day in Panama Canal

Here is one way the planner could shape an opening day. Your version changes with your dates, pace, and who you are traveling with.

  1. 9:00 AM

    Start near Miraflores

    Use Miraflores to get oriented without asking the trip to cover too much ground.

  2. 11:00 AM

    Miraflores Locks

    Give the first major highlight enough time to make Panama Canal feel real from the start.

  3. 1:30 PM

    Local lunch

    Make time for a Panamanian lunch in Casco Viejo so the first meal becomes part of the destination instead of a blank gap.
