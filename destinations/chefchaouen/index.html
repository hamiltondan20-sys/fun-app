Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Chefchaouen?

Spring and fall Chefchaouen is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Chefchaouen?

Medina, Ras El Maa, or a nearby practical base Use Medina as the first base when possible, then add Ras El Maa only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Chefchaouen?

Blue Medina, a local food experience, and the trip's main day outing Protect Blue Medina, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Chefchaouen feel like a trip.
