Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Kilimanjaro?

Moshi or Mountain gate Use Moshi as the first base when possible, then add Mountain gate only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Kilimanjaro?

Kilimanjaro routes and Moshi Protect Kilimanjaro routes, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Kilimanjaro feel like a trip.

A first day in Kilimanjaro

Here is one way the planner could shape an opening day. Your version changes with your dates, pace, and who you are traveling with.

  1. 9:00 AM

    Start near Moshi

    Use Moshi to get oriented without asking the trip to cover too much ground.

  2. 11:00 AM

    Kilimanjaro routes

    Give the first major highlight enough time to make Kilimanjaro feel real from the start.

  3. 1:30 PM

    Local lunch

    Make time for a pre-climb lodge dinner so the first meal becomes part of the destination instead of a blank gap.
