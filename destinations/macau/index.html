Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Macau?

Historic Centre, Cotai, or a nearby base Start with Historic Centre when you want the easiest access to the first few days, then use Cotai or Taipa Village when the mood of the trip calls for something different.

How do I get around Macau?

Group days around Historic Centre and Cotai Macau often feels strongest when the old center and resort side are treated as separate moods, not one overloaded route.

What should I book in advance for Macau?

Macau historic center or old town and one food experience Protect the moments that define Macau, then leave room for meals, weather changes, and local recommendations.
