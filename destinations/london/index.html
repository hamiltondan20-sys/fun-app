London

For travelers who want museums, neighborhoods, and classic London atmosphere with enough flexibility for weather and wandering.

Best for
Museum lovers, repeat Europe travelers, history fans, and users who like trips with both structure and flexibility.
Ideal trip length
4 days for a first pass, longer if the trip is meant to include theater, day trips, or slower neighborhood wandering.
How to plan it
London usually gets better when users let one area shape the day and treat extra landmarks as optional rather than mandatory.

London works especially well for travelers who like city trips with range. It can be museum-heavy, food-focused, historic, green, theatrical, or relaxed depending on how the days are shaped. That flexibility is one of its best qualities.

The key is not treating London like a landmark sprint. The city tends to feel richer when users give neighborhoods time to breathe, let museums share space with parks or pubs, and avoid the instinct to cross town just because transit makes it technically possible.
