Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Sardinia?

Olbia or Alghero Use Olbia as the first base when possible, then add Alghero only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Sardinia?

Costa Smeralda and La Pelosa Protect Costa Smeralda, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Sardinia feel like a trip.

A first day in Sardinia

Here is one way the planner could shape an opening day. Your version changes with your dates, pace, and who you are traveling with.

  1. 9:00 AM

    Start near Olbia

    Use Olbia to get oriented without asking the trip to cover too much ground.

  2. 11:00 AM

    Costa Smeralda

    Give the first major highlight enough time to make Sardinia feel real from the start.

  3. 1:30 PM

    Local lunch

    Make time for a Sardinian seafood or pasta dinner so the first meal becomes part of the destination instead of a blank gap.
