Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Abu Dhabi?

Cooler months Abu Dhabi is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Abu Dhabi?

Saadiyat, Corniche, or Yas Island Use Saadiyat Island as the first base when possible, then add Corniche only if the extra move is worth the time.

How do I get around Abu Dhabi?

Taxi, rideshare, and planned transfers Abu Dhabi rewards a slower, zone-based plan that leaves room for the mosque, museums, beach, and an easy evening.

What should I book in advance for Abu Dhabi?

Museum time, theme parks, and desert outings Protect Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Abu Dhabi feel like a trip.
