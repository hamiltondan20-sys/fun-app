Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Granada?

Spring and fall Granada is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Granada?

Albaicin, Realejo, or a nearby practical base Use Albaicin as the first base when possible, then add Realejo only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Granada?

Alhambra, a local food experience, and the trip's main day outing Protect Alhambra, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Granada feel like a trip.
