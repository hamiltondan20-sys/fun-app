Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Bologna?

Spring and fall Bologna is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Bologna?

Centro Storico, Quadrilatero, or a nearby practical base Use Centro Storico as the first base when possible, then add Quadrilatero only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Bologna?

Piazza Maggiore, a local food experience, and the trip's main day outing Protect Piazza Maggiore, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Bologna feel like a trip.
