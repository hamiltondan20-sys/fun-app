Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Norwegian Fjords?

Bergen or Flam Use Bergen as the first base when possible, then add Flam only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Norwegian Fjords?

Geirangerfjord and Nareyfjord Protect Geirangerfjord, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Norwegian Fjords feel like a trip.
