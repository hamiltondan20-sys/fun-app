Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Great Wall of China?

Mutianyu or Jinshanling Use Mutianyu as the first base when possible, then add Jinshanling only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Great Wall of China?

Mutianyu and Jinshanling Protect Mutianyu, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Great Wall of China feel like a trip.
