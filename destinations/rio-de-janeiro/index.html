Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Rio de Janeiro?

Copacabana or Ipanema Use Copacabana as the first base when possible, then add Ipanema only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Rio de Janeiro?

Christ the Redeemer and Sugarloaf Mountain Protect Christ the Redeemer, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Rio de Janeiro feel like a trip.

A first day in Rio de Janeiro

Here is one way the planner could shape an opening day. Your version changes with your dates, pace, and who you are traveling with.

  1. 9:00 AM

    Start near Copacabana

    Use Copacabana to get oriented without asking the trip to cover too much ground.

  2. 11:00 AM

    Christ the Redeemer

    Give the first major highlight enough time to make Rio de Janeiro feel real from the start.

  3. 1:30 PM

    Local lunch

    Make time for a Brazilian grill or beachside seafood meal so the first meal becomes part of the destination instead of a blank gap.
