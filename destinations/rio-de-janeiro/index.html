Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Closing days for Rio de Janeiro

Wednesdays and Sun–Mon Museu do Amanhã publishes Thursday-to-Tuesday hours and the Museu do Jardim Botânico is closed on Wednesdays. Oro is closed on Sunday and Monday, and La Bicyclette's Leblon branch closes on Mondays.

Tickets and booking for Rio de Janeiro

Buy ahead online The Jardim Botânico asks you to buy tickets in advance through its ticketing site, and AquaRio offers a discount for online booking. Trem do Corcovado sells tickets online, by phone and through its app.

Reservations for Rio de Janeiro

Book dinners Lasai serves only by reservation ('Só atendemos mediante reserva'), with seatings at 20:00 Tuesday to Friday. Oro takes bookings through Meitre or by phone.

Visitor rules for Rio de Janeiro

Bags and children At MAM Rio, items larger than 45 cm × 30 cm cannot enter the galleries, children under 12 must be with an adult, and food and drink stay out of exhibition spaces.

You can reach Rio's two classic viewpoints with tickets bought online. Trem do Corcovado runs from Rua Cosme Velho, 513 and sells tickets through its site, app and phone line; children up to 6 ride free on a guardian's lap. The Santuário Cristo Redentor marked the statue's 95th anniversary in October 2026. Bondinho Pão de Açúcar leaves from Av. Pasteur, 520 in Urca, runs from 08:30 on weekdays and 07:30 at weekends, and stops boarding at 19:30.

Museu do Amanhã, on Praça Mauá, opens Thursday to Tuesday from 10h to 18h and is marking its tenth anniversary with an exhibition and a new outdoor sculpture garden. Nearby, CCBB Rio shows 'Amazônicas – Poéticas Femininas' until 16 November and 'Palavras em Cena' until 31 December. MAM Rio in Parque do Flamengo is free and open Wednesday to Sunday. You can also see the reading room of the Real Gabinete Português de Leitura, which opens on weekdays from 10h to 17h.

For greener days, the Jardim Botânico's arboretum opens Thursday to Tuesday from 8h, but only from 11h on Wednesdays, when its museum is closed; you should buy tickets in advance. Parque Lage, at Rua Jardim Botânico, 414, runs a busy arts programme listed on its own site. Parque Nacional da Tijuca opens daily from 8h to 17h. If you are travelling with children, AquaRio in Gamboa opens every day from 9h, with last entry at 16h.

For dinner, Lasai in Humaitá serves a single tasting menu and takes reservations only. Oro in Leblon offers 'Criatividade' and 'Afetividade' menus and is closed on Sundays and Mondays. Aprazível in Santa Teresa takes online bookings, and Churrascaria Palace in Copacabana is celebrating 75 years of rodízio. For drinks, try the house mixology at Spirit Copa Bar or the caipirinhas at Academia da Cachaça in Leblon. For a slower morning, La Bicyclette opens at 8h, and Curto Café pours coffee in Centro on weekdays.

A first day in Rio de Janeiro

Sample-day suggestions have not completed a separate source review. Check opening times and travel times before using this itinerary.

Here is one way the planner could shape an opening day. Your version changes with your dates, pace, and who you are traveling with.

  1. 9:00 AM

    Start near Copacabana

    Use Copacabana to get oriented without asking the trip to cover too much ground.

  2. 11:00 AM

    Christ the Redeemer

    Give the first major highlight enough time to make Rio de Janeiro feel real from the start.

  3. 1:30 PM

    Local lunch

    Make time for a Brazilian grill or beachside seafood meal so the first meal becomes part of the destination instead of a blank gap.

Planning tip

Avoid Wednesday mornings for Museu do Amanhã and the Jardim Botânico: the museum publishes Thursday-to-Tuesday hours, the garden's museum is closed on Wednesdays and its arboretum opens only at 11h that day.
