Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Delhi?

Cooler months Delhi is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Delhi?

Central Delhi, Aerocity, or South Delhi Use New Delhi as the first base when possible, then add Old Delhi only if the extra move is worth the time.

How do I get around Delhi?

Metro with planned rides Delhi needs breathing room between major sights, heat, traffic, and meals; a focused neighborhood plan makes the city much more rewarding.

What should I book in advance for Delhi?

A food walk, major monuments, and an Agra connection Protect Old Delhi, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Delhi feel like a trip.
