Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Shanghai?

Spring and fall Shanghai is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Shanghai?

French Concession, Jing'an, or near the Bund Use French Concession as the first base when possible, then add The Bund only if the extra move is worth the time.

How do I get around Shanghai?

Metro-first with short rideshares Shanghai works best when the plan lets old streets, skyline moments, and meals each have their own space.

What should I book in advance for Shanghai?

Observation decks, museums, and destination restaurants Protect The Bund, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Shanghai feel like a trip.
