Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Arizona?

Sedona or Page Use Sedona as the first base when possible, then add Page only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Arizona?

Monument Valley and Antelope Canyon Protect Monument Valley, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Arizona feel like a trip.

A first day in Arizona

Here is one way the planner could shape an opening day. Your version changes with your dates, pace, and who you are traveling with.

  1. 9:00 AM

    Start near Sedona

    Use Sedona to get oriented without asking the trip to cover too much ground.

  2. 11:00 AM

    Monument Valley

    Give the first major highlight enough time to make Arizona feel real from the start.

  3. 1:30 PM

    Local lunch

    Make time for a Southwestern dinner or roadside cafe so the first meal becomes part of the destination instead of a blank gap.
