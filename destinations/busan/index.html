Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Busan?

Spring, fall, and beach season Busan is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Busan?

Haeundae, Gwangalli, or Nampo Use Haeundae as the first base when possible, then add Nampo only if the extra move is worth the time.

How do I get around Busan?

Metro, buses, and occasional taxi Busan is easier when the day stays on one side of the city and the coast, market, or neighborhood carries the mood.

What should I book in advance for Busan?

A seafood table and any coastal rail or boat outing Protect Haeundae Beach, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Busan feel like a trip.
