Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Cartagena?

Dry season and shoulder months Cartagena is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Cartagena?

Walled City, Getsemani, or Bocagrande Use Walled City as the first base when possible, then add Getsemani only if the extra move is worth the time.

How do I get around Cartagena?

Walking, taxis, and boat transfers Cartagena feels better when the plan protects shaded midday time and separates old-city wandering from the beach or boat day.

What should I book in advance for Cartagena?

Rosario Islands, rooftop dinners, and airport transfers Protect Walled City, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Cartagena feel like a trip.
