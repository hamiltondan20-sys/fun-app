A first day in Mount Fuji

Here is one way the planner could shape an opening day. Your version changes with your dates, pace, and who you are traveling with.

    Start near Lake Kawaguchi

  2. 11:00 AM

    Give the first major highlight enough time to make Mount Fuji feel real from the start.

  3. 1:30 PM

    Local lunch

    Make time for a local noodle lunch or ryokan dinner so the first meal becomes part of the destination instead of a blank gap.
