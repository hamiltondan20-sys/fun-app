Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Mumbai?

Cooler dry-season months Mumbai is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Mumbai?

Colaba, Fort, or Bandra Use Colaba as the first base when possible, then add Fort only if the extra move is worth the time.

How do I get around Mumbai?

Walking pockets, trains for confident travelers, and rideshares Mumbai is strongest when the itinerary follows the city's neighborhoods and food instead of trying to cover the whole region in one day.

What should I book in advance for Mumbai?

A food tour and any hard-to-get restaurant Protect Gateway of India, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Mumbai feel like a trip.
