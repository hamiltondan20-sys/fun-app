Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Samarkand?

Spring and fall Samarkand is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Samarkand?

Registan, Old City, or a nearby practical base Use Registan as the first base when possible, then add Old City only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Samarkand?

Registan Square, a local food experience, and the trip's main day outing Protect Registan Square, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Samarkand feel like a trip.
