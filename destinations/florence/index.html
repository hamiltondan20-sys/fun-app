Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Florence?

Spring and fall Florence is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Florence?

Duomo, Santa Croce, or Oltrarno Use Duomo as the first base when possible, then add Oltrarno only if the extra move is worth the time.

How do I get around Florence?

Walking with short taxi help Florence feels better when one major museum or landmark anchors the day and the rest is left open for streets, food, and viewpoints.

What should I book in advance for Florence?

Uffizi, Accademia, and a Tuscan day trip Protect Uffizi Gallery, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Florence feel like a trip.
