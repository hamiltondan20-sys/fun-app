Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Thessaloniki?

Spring, fall, and mild winter Thessaloniki is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Thessaloniki?

Ladadika, Ano Poli, or a nearby practical base Use Ladadika as the first base when possible, then add Ano Poli only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Thessaloniki?

Waterfront promenade, a local food experience, and the trip's main day outing Protect Waterfront promenade, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Thessaloniki feel like a trip.
