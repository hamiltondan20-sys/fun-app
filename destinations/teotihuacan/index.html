Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Teotihuacan?

Pyramid of the Sun or Pyramid of the Moon Use Pyramid of the Sun as the first base when possible, then add Pyramid of the Moon only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Teotihuacan?

Pyramid of the Sun and Pyramid of the Moon Protect Pyramid of the Sun, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Teotihuacan feel like a trip.

A first day in Teotihuacan

Here is one way the planner could shape an opening day. Your version changes with your dates, pace, and who you are traveling with.

  1. 9:00 AM

    Start near Pyramid of the Sun

    Use Pyramid of the Sun to get oriented without asking the trip to cover too much ground.

  2. 11:00 AM

    Pyramid of the Sun

    Give the first major highlight enough time to make Teotihuacan feel real from the start.

  3. 1:30 PM

    Local lunch

    Make time for a local lunch near the archaeological zone so the first meal becomes part of the destination instead of a blank gap.
