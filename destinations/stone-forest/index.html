Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Stone Forest?

Shilin park or Kunming Use Shilin park as the first base when possible, then add Kunming only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Stone Forest?

Shilin Stone Forest and Yi culture villages Protect Shilin Stone Forest, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Stone Forest feel like a trip.

A first day in Stone Forest

Here is one way the planner could shape an opening day. Your version changes with your dates, pace, and who you are traveling with.

  1. 9:00 AM

    Start near Shilin park

    Use Shilin park to get oriented without asking the trip to cover too much ground.

  2. 11:00 AM

    Shilin Stone Forest

    Give the first major highlight enough time to make Stone Forest feel real from the start.

  3. 1:30 PM

    Local lunch

    Make time for a Yunnan mushroom or noodle meal so the first meal becomes part of the destination instead of a blank gap.
