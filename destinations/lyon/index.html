Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Lyon?

Spring and fall Lyon is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Lyon?

Presqu'ile, Vieux Lyon, or Croix-Rousse Use Vieux Lyon as the first base when possible, then add Presqu'ile only if the extra move is worth the time.

How do I get around Lyon?

Metro, funicular, and walking Lyon rewards travelers who plan around meals and neighborhoods instead of treating it like a checklist of monuments.

What should I book in advance for Lyon?

A special bouchon meal and Fourviere access Protect Vieux Lyon, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Lyon feel like a trip.
