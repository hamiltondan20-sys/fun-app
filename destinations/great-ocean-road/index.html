Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Great Ocean Road?

Torquay or Apollo Bay Use Torquay as the first base when possible, then add Apollo Bay only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Great Ocean Road?

Twelve Apostles and Great Otway National Park Protect Twelve Apostles, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Great Ocean Road feel like a trip.

A first day in Great Ocean Road

Here is one way the planner could shape an opening day. Your version changes with your dates, pace, and who you are traveling with.

  1. 9:00 AM

    Start near Torquay

    Use Torquay to get oriented without asking the trip to cover too much ground.

  2. 11:00 AM

    Twelve Apostles

    Give the first major highlight enough time to make Great Ocean Road feel real from the start.

  3. 1:30 PM

    Local lunch

    Make time for a coastal pub lunch or local bakery so the first meal becomes part of the destination instead of a blank gap.
