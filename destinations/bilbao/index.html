Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Bilbao?

Spring through early fall Bilbao is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Bilbao?

Casco Viejo, Ensanche, or a nearby practical base Use Casco Viejo as the first base when possible, then add Ensanche only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Bilbao?

Guggenheim Museum, a local food experience, and the trip's main day outing Protect Guggenheim Museum, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Bilbao feel like a trip.
