Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Solovetsky Islands?

Solovki village or Monastery grounds Use Solovki village as the first base when possible, then add Monastery grounds only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Solovetsky Islands?

Solovetsky Monastery and White Sea coast Protect Solovetsky Monastery, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Solovetsky Islands feel like a trip.
