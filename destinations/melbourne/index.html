Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Melbourne?

Spring, fall, and mild summer days Melbourne is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Melbourne?

CBD laneways, Fitzroy, or a nearby practical base Use CBD laneways as the first base when possible, then add Fitzroy only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Melbourne?

Federation Square, a local food experience, and the trip's main day outing Protect Federation Square, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Melbourne feel like a trip.
