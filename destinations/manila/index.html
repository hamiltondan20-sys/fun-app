Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Manila?

Dry season and flexible shoulder months Manila is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Manila?

Intramuros, Makati, or a nearby practical base Use Intramuros as the first base when possible, then add Makati only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Manila?

Intramuros, a local food experience, and the trip's main day outing Protect Intramuros, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Manila feel like a trip.
