Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Angkor Wat?

Angkor Wat or Angkor Thom Use Angkor Wat as the first base when possible, then add Angkor Thom only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Angkor Wat?

Angkor Wat and Bayon Protect Angkor Wat, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Angkor Wat feel like a trip.
