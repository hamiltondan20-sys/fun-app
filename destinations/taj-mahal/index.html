Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Taj Mahal?

Taj Ganj or Agra Fort side Use Taj Ganj as the first base when possible, then add Agra Fort side only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Taj Mahal?

Taj Mahal and Agra Fort Protect Taj Mahal, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Taj Mahal feel like a trip.

A first day in Taj Mahal

Here is one way the planner could shape an opening day. Your version changes with your dates, pace, and who you are traveling with.

  1. 9:00 AM

    Start near Taj Ganj

    Use Taj Ganj to get oriented without asking the trip to cover too much ground.

  2. 11:00 AM

    Taj Mahal

    Give the first major highlight enough time to make Taj Mahal feel real from the start.

  3. 1:30 PM

    Local lunch

    Make time for a North Indian lunch near the old city so the first meal becomes part of the destination instead of a blank gap.
