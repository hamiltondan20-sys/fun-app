Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Naples?

Spring and fall Naples is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Naples?

Centro Storico, Chiaia, or Vomero Use Centro Storico as the first base when possible, then add Chiaia only if the extra move is worth the time.

How do I get around Naples?

Metro, funicular, walking, and trains Naples is not about smoothing every edge; the best plan gives the city a focused food and history day, then uses it as a base for one major outing.

What should I book in advance for Naples?

Pompeii, key museums, and a memorable pizza table Protect Historic Center, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Naples feel like a trip.
