Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Cape Town?

City Bowl or V&A Waterfront Use City Bowl as the first base when possible, then add V&A Waterfront only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Cape Town?

Table Mountain and Cape of Good Hope Protect Table Mountain, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Cape Town feel like a trip.

A first day in Cape Town

Here is one way the planner could shape an opening day. Your version changes with your dates, pace, and who you are traveling with.

  1. 9:00 AM

    Start near City Bowl

    Use City Bowl to get oriented without asking the trip to cover too much ground.

  2. 11:00 AM

    Table Mountain

    Give the first major highlight enough time to make Cape Town feel real from the start.

  3. 1:30 PM

    Local lunch

    Make time for a Cape Malay or waterfront dinner so the first meal becomes part of the destination instead of a blank gap.
