Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Venice?

San Marco or Dorsoduro Use San Marco as the first base when possible, then add Dorsoduro only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Venice?

St. Mark's Square and Grand Canal Protect St. Mark's Square, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Venice feel like a trip.

A first day in Venice

Here is one way the planner could shape an opening day. Your version changes with your dates, pace, and who you are traveling with.

  1. 9:00 AM

    Start near San Marco

    Use San Marco to get oriented without asking the trip to cover too much ground.

  2. 11:00 AM

    St. Mark's Square

    Give the first major highlight enough time to make Venice feel real from the start.

  3. 1:30 PM

    Local lunch

    Make time for a cicchetti crawl or lagoon-side dinner so the first meal becomes part of the destination instead of a blank gap.
