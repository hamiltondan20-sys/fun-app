A first day in Chichen Itza

Here is one way the planner could shape an opening day. Your version changes with your dates, pace, and who you are traveling with.

    Start near Chichen Itza entrance

    El Castillo

    Give the first major highlight enough time to make Chichen Itza feel real from the start.

    Local lunch
