Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Yellowstone?

Old Faithful or Canyon Village Use Old Faithful as the first base when possible, then add Canyon Village only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Yellowstone?

Old Faithful and Grand Prismatic Spring Protect Old Faithful, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Yellowstone feel like a trip.

A first day in Yellowstone

Here is one way the planner could shape an opening day. Your version changes with your dates, pace, and who you are traveling with.

  1. 9:00 AM

    Start near Old Faithful

    Use Old Faithful to get oriented without asking the trip to cover too much ground.

  2. 11:00 AM

    Old Faithful

    Give the first major highlight enough time to make Yellowstone feel real from the start.

  3. 1:30 PM

    Local lunch

    Make time for a picnic with a warm lodge meal so the first meal becomes part of the destination instead of a blank gap.
