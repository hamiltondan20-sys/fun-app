Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Phuket?

Dry season beach weather Phuket is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Phuket?

Kata, Kamala, or Old Phuket Town Use Old Town as the first base when possible, then add Kata only if the extra move is worth the time.

How do I get around Phuket?

Rideshares, taxis, and boat transfers Phuket works better when beach recovery days are kept separate from boat and island excursions.

What should I book in advance for Phuket?

Boat trips, airport transfers, and sunset tables Protect Old Phuket Town, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Phuket feel like a trip.
