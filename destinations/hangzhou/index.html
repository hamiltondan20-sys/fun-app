Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Hangzhou?

Spring and fall Hangzhou is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Hangzhou?

West Lake, Hefang Street, or a nearby practical base Use West Lake as the first base when possible, then add Hefang Street only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Hangzhou?

West Lake, a local food experience, and the trip's main day outing Protect West Lake, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Hangzhou feel like a trip.
