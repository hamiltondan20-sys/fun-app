Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Hong Kong?

Central or Tsim Sha Tsui Use Central as the first base when possible, then add Tsim Sha Tsui only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Hong Kong?

Victoria Peak and Star Ferry Protect Victoria Peak, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Hong Kong feel like a trip.

A first day in Hong Kong

Here is one way the planner could shape an opening day. Your version changes with your dates, pace, and who you are traveling with.

  1. 9:00 AM

    Start near Central

    Use Central to get oriented without asking the trip to cover too much ground.

  2. 11:00 AM

    Victoria Peak

    Give the first major highlight enough time to make Hong Kong feel real from the start.

  3. 1:30 PM

    Local lunch

    Make time for a dim sum lunch or dai pai dong-style food stop so the first meal becomes part of the destination instead of a blank gap.
