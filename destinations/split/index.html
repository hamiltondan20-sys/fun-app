Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Split?

Late spring through early fall Split is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Split?

Old Town, Bacvice, or a nearby practical base Use Old Town as the first base when possible, then add Bacvice only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Split?

Diocletian's Palace, a local food experience, and the trip's main day outing Protect Diocletian's Palace, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Split feel like a trip.
