Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Machu Picchu?

Aguas Calientes or Citadel approach Use Aguas Calientes as the first base when possible, then add Citadel approach only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Machu Picchu?

Machu Picchu Citadel and Huayna Picchu Protect Machu Picchu Citadel, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Machu Picchu feel like a trip.

A first day in Machu Picchu

Here is one way the planner could shape an opening day. Your version changes with your dates, pace, and who you are traveling with.

  1. 9:00 AM

    Start near Aguas Calientes

    Use Aguas Calientes to get oriented without asking the trip to cover too much ground.

  2. 11:00 AM

    Machu Picchu Citadel

    Give the first major highlight enough time to make Machu Picchu feel real from the start.

  3. 1:30 PM

    Local lunch

    Make time for a Peruvian tasting menu or Andean market lunch so the first meal becomes part of the destination instead of a blank gap.
