Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Easter Island?

Hanga Roa or Rano Raraku Use Hanga Roa as the first base when possible, then add Rano Raraku only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Easter Island?

Ahu Tongariki and Rano Raraku Protect Ahu Tongariki, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Easter Island feel like a trip.

A first day in Easter Island

Here is one way the planner could shape an opening day. Your version changes with your dates, pace, and who you are traveling with.

  1. 9:00 AM

    Start near Hanga Roa

    Use Hanga Roa to get oriented without asking the trip to cover too much ground.

  2. 11:00 AM

    Ahu Tongariki

    Give the first major highlight enough time to make Easter Island feel real from the start.

  3. 1:30 PM

    Local lunch

    Make time for a Polynesian seafood dinner in Hanga Roa so the first meal becomes part of the destination instead of a blank gap.
