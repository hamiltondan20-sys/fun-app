A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Danxia Landforms?

Zhangye or Danxia park Use Zhangye as the first base when possible, then add Danxia park only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Danxia Landforms?

Rainbow Mountains and Zhangye Danxia viewpoints Protect Rainbow Mountains, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Danxia Landforms feel like a trip.
