Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Krabi?

Dry season beach weather Krabi is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Krabi?

Ao Nang, Railay, or a nearby practical base Use Ao Nang as the first base when possible, then add Railay only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Krabi?

Railay Beach, a local food experience, and the trip's main day outing Protect Railay Beach, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Krabi feel like a trip.
