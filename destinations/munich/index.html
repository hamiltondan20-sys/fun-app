Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Munich?

Late spring through fall Munich is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Munich?

Altstadt, Maxvorstadt, or Haidhausen Use Altstadt as the first base when possible, then add Maxvorstadt only if the extra move is worth the time.

How do I get around Munich?

U-Bahn, tram, and walking Munich makes a good base when city highlights and one scenic day are balanced instead of stacking several long excursions.

What should I book in advance for Munich?

Popular beer halls, palace visits, and any alpine day trip Protect Marienplatz, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Munich feel like a trip.
