Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Alexandria?

Corniche, Bibliotheca side, or a nearby base Start with Corniche when you want the easiest access to the first few days, then use Bibliotheca side or Old Harbor when the mood of the trip calls for something different.

How do I get around Alexandria?

Group days around Corniche and Bibliotheca side Alexandria often works best when the waterfront and one heritage stretch carry the day instead of trying to cover too much city ground.

What should I book in advance for Alexandria?

Alexandria harbor or waterfront walk and one food experience Protect the moments that define Alexandria, then leave room for meals, weather changes, and local recommendations.
