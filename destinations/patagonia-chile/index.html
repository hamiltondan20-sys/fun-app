Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Patagonia?

El Calafate, El Chalten, or a nearby base Start with El Calafate when you want the easiest access to the first few days, then use El Chalten or Torres del Paine when the mood of the trip calls for something different.

How do I get around Patagonia?

Group days around El Calafate and El Chalten Patagonia works best when the trip stays focused around El Calafate and El Chalten, with enough room for the local food and experiences that make Patagonia feel different from a checklist stop.

What should I book in advance for Patagonia?

Perito Moreno Glacier and one food experience Protect the moments that define Patagonia, then leave room for meals, weather changes, and local recommendations.
