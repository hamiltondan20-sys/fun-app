Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Prague?

Spring, fall, and festive winter Prague is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Prague?

Old Town, Mala Strana, or Vinohrady Use Old Town as the first base when possible, then add Mala Strana only if the extra move is worth the time.

How do I get around Prague?

Walking, trams, and metro Prague feels best before the center gets busy and after the day slows down, so protect early walks and relaxed evenings.

What should I book in advance for Prague?

Castle timing and any concert or special dinner Protect Charles Bridge, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Prague feel like a trip.
