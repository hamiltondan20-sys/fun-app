Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Vancouver?

Late spring through early fall Vancouver is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Vancouver?

Downtown, Gastown, or a nearby practical base Use Downtown as the first base when possible, then add Gastown only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Vancouver?

Stanley Park, a local food experience, and the trip's main day outing Protect Stanley Park, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Vancouver feel like a trip.
