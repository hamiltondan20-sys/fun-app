Beijing

For travelers who want major history, cultural scale, and a city where the biggest sights truly deserve time.

Best for
History-focused travelers, first-time China trips, and users who like major landmarks with real cultural weight.
Ideal trip length
4 days for a strong first visit, especially if one day is set aside for the Great Wall.
How to plan it
Beijing improves when users assume fewer major sights per day and give the city time to feel expansive rather than rushed.

Beijing is one of those cities where scale matters. The landmarks are not just famous. They are genuinely large, historically heavy, and time-consuming in the best possible way. That means a Beijing trip rarely improves by trying to squeeze in too much.

A better approach is to accept that one major sight may be enough for part of a day, then let the rest of the time go toward hutongs, meals, parks, or a slower evening. Once the pacing respects the city, Beijing starts to feel impressive instead of demanding.
