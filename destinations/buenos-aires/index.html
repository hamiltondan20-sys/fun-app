Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Buenos Aires?

Spring and fall Buenos Aires is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Buenos Aires?

Palermo, Recoleta, or San Telmo Use Palermo as the first base when possible, then add Recoleta only if the extra move is worth the time.

How do I get around Buenos Aires?

Walking pockets and rideshares Buenos Aires runs later than many travelers expect, so the plan should leave room for long dinners, a slower afternoon, and evening energy.

What should I book in advance for Buenos Aires?

Tango, standout steak, and popular cultural events Protect Recoleta Cemetery, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Buenos Aires feel like a trip.
