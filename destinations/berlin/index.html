Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Berlin?

Late spring through early fall Berlin is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Berlin?

Mitte, Kreuzberg, or Prenzlauer Berg Use Mitte as the first base when possible, then add Kreuzberg only if the extra move is worth the time.

How do I get around Berlin?

U-Bahn, S-Bahn, and walking Berlin rewards travelers who choose a few districts and let the city's art, food, and history fill the spaces between major sights.

What should I book in advance for Berlin?

Museum Island, a history tour, and one standout meal Protect Museum Island, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Berlin feel like a trip.
