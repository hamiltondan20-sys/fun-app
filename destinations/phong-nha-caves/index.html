Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Phong Nha Caves?

Phong Nha village or Son River Use Phong Nha village as the first base when possible, then add Son River only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Phong Nha Caves?

Phong Nha Cave and Paradise Cave Protect Phong Nha Cave, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Phong Nha Caves feel like a trip.
