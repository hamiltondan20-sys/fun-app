Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

When is the best time to visit Chiang Mai?

Cooler dry-season months Chiang Mai is easier to enjoy when weather, crowds, and the trip's main outdoor moments are planned together.

Where should I stay in Chiang Mai?

Old City, Nimman, or Riverside Use Old City as the first base when possible, then add Nimman only if the extra move is worth the time.

How do I get around Chiang Mai?

Walking, rideshares, and day-trip vans Chiang Mai rewards slow mornings, good meals, and one thoughtful outing more than a packed temple count.

What should I book in advance for Chiang Mai?

A cooking class and ethical nature outings Protect Old City temples, then leave space around it for meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Chiang Mai feel like a trip.
