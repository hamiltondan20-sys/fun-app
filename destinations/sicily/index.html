Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Sicily?

Palermo or Taormina Use Palermo as the first base when possible, then add Taormina only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Sicily?

Valley of the Temples and Mount Etna Protect Valley of the Temples, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Sicily feel like a trip.

A first day in Sicily

Here is one way the planner could shape an opening day. Your version changes with your dates, pace, and who you are traveling with.

  1. 9:00 AM

    Start near Palermo

    Use Palermo to get oriented without asking the trip to cover too much ground.

  2. 11:00 AM

    Valley of the Temples

    Give the first major highlight enough time to make Sicily feel real from the start.

  3. 1:30 PM

    Local lunch

    Make time for a Sicilian market lunch or seafood dinner so the first meal becomes part of the destination instead of a blank gap.
