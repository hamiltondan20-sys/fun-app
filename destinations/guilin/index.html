Before you book

A few practical details can make the whole trip feel easier.

Where should I stay in Guilin?

Guilin or Yangshuo Use Guilin as the first base when possible, then add Yangshuo only if the extra move is worth the time.

What should I book in advance for Guilin?

Li River cruise and Yangshuo Protect Li River cruise, then leave space around it for the meals, weather changes, and slower moments that make Guilin and the Li River feel like a trip.

Where should I stay in Guilin?

Two Rivers area, Elephant Trunk Hill side, or a nearby base Start with Two Rivers area when you want the easiest access to the first few days, then use Elephant Trunk Hill side or Reed Flute Cave side when the mood of the trip calls for something different.

How do I get around Guilin?

Group days around Two Rivers area and Elephant Trunk Hill side Guilin often works best when the scenery is allowed to carry the day instead of forcing too many extra stops around it.
