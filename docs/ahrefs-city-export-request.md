## Export steps

1. In Keywords Explorer choose Google and United States for every row.
2. Paste the 60 keywords below and export all rows, with no volume/KD filter.
3. Include Keyword, Volume, KD, Parent Topic, Parent Topic Volume if available,
   Traffic Potential and SERP update date. Record country and export date.
   Global Volume and volume trend are optional secondary context.
4. If Parent Topic Volume is absent, send the original export first. Bulk-run
   its distinct Parent Topic keywords in the SAME country and export Volume
   as a supplemental file. Do not substitute Traffic Potential.
5. Attach the CSV here. No credentials or account access are needed.

Each city requires monthly volume and KD for all three queries, plus each
query's parent-topic name and monthly volume. Missing values remain unknown,
not zero. Volume is a 12-month average in the selected country. Traffic Potential
is the leading result's estimated organic traffic across keywords, not parent
keyword volume. Parent Topic is inferred from the leading result's main query.
KD is a difficulty proxy, not a ranking or traffic guarantee.
