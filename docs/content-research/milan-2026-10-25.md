## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several major sites could not be read by automated fetch (HTTP 403 for Castello Sforzesco, Museo del Novecento, Fondazione Prada, Palazzo Reale, Triennale, GAM and the City of Milan's Parco Sempione page; certificate or TLS errors for Bar Basso, Pizzeria Spontini, Princi and Cova; repeated HTTP 429 for Pasticceria Cucchi, Iginio Massari and Cafezal; timeout for Pasticceria Marchesi), so they are left out rather than judged closed. The Duomo's ticket site sits behind a queue system that was not bypassed, so cathedral and terrace opening hours and dress rules were not confirmed; the main site's weekday hours may refer to the office. Weekly hours were not published on the pages read for Pinacoteca Ambrosiana, Villa Necchi Campiglio, Museo Diocesano, MUBA, Peck and Langosteria (Via Savona), and Museo Poldi Pezzoli showed only today's hours. Camparino, Dry Milano, Moebius, Luini, Antica Trattoria della Pesa, Masuelli and Biffi showed current hours or booking but no 2026-dated content. Two fetch summaries (museoscienza.org and trattoriamilanese.it) reported text addressed to an AI that could not be quoted and appeared to echo the review prompt; neither place is used.
