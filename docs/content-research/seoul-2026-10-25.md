## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Palace opening hours for Gyeongbokgung, Changdeokgung and Deoksugung were not on the pages read, and neither were regular hours for Leeum Museum of Art or Seoul Arts Center; SEA LIFE COEX Aquarium showed only a same-day snapshot. Jungsik, Hadongkwan, 1914 Lounge & Bar and both Fritz Coffee branches publish addresses and hours or booking links but no dated 2026 content, so they are rated medium. Many input places could not be confirmed: Gwangjang Market, Tosokchon and Myeongdong Kyoja had certificate errors; Charles H and Anthracite returned 403; London Bagel Museum's domain did not resolve; Mingles and Lotte World returned no readable content; N Seoul Tower's pages had an encoding error; Bar Cham and Center Coffee have no official websites; and Alice Cheongdam, Maison M.O and Breadypost could not be identified. Coverage of bakeries and cocktail bars therefore relies on hotel venues.
