## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and ticket details do not guarantee availability. The ASI Agra Circle monument pages carry a 2013 copyright and some outdated notices, so their sunrise-to-sunset hours and fees are attributed rather than treated as current; tajmahal.gov.in is undated, and its night-viewing hours (8:00 pm to 11:59 pm) conflict with ASI's (8:30 pm to 12:30 am), so night viewing is not detailed in the copy. Opening hours were not published for the ITC Mughal outlets, Trident Agra, Grand Mercure Agra outlets or Panchhi Petha stores. Esphahan's page lists both 7 am to 10:30 pm and a weekend lunch slot while the hotel FAQ says dinner. Sites that couldn't be read: tajhotels.com, radissonhotels.com and hilton.com (403), sarovarhotels.com (429), pinchofspice.in (failed fetch), dasaprakashagra.com (does not resolve), and Wildlife SOS visit pages (404). Sheroes Hangout, Kalakriti and Bhagat Halwai domains now point elsewhere. Marriott pages began returning 403 late in the review after the Courtyard and Fairfield dining pages had been read.
