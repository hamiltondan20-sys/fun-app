## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Le Lion and Elbgold returned HTTP 429 on every attempt; Rindermarkthalle St. Pauli, Brücke 10 and Bäckerei Junge failed with TLS errors; Boilerman Bar had a certificate mismatch; Dat Backhus and Museum der Arbeit refused connections; Public Coffee Roasters, Daniel Wischer and The Chug Club domains did not resolve. Weekly hours were not readable for Hamburger Kunsthalle, Museum für Kunst und Gewerbe, Tierpark Hagenbeck, Fischereihafen Restaurant, clouds, Skyline Bar 20up, Rickmer Rickmers, Cap San Diego, Chocoversum (museum), Alster-Touristik and the HADAG ferries (timetables via HVV). Old Commercial Room, Zeit für Brot, Nord Coast, Fischauktionshalle, HADAG and Cap San Diego showed no dated 2026 item beyond hours, bookings or copyright lines, so they are marked medium confidence.
