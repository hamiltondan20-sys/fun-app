## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. The Hassan II Mosque's paid-visits page, which carries a seasonal timetable, could not be read (redirect loop), so tour times come from the foundation's home page and may change with the season. The national museums' pages say both 'lundi au dimanche' and 'Fermé le mardi'; this guide follows the Tuesday closure. Hotel venues at the Sofitel Casablanca Tour Blanche, Le Doge and the Mövenpick, and Le Cabestan, Acajou and Le Jasmine, publish no opening hours; the Sofitel, Le Doge and Mövenpick venue pages carry no 2026 date. Bondi Coffee Kitchen's site shows no explicit 2026 date. Morocco Mall lists Aquadream and other attractions without hours or prices. Sites that could not be read: Villa des Arts (403), Four Seasons, Hyatt Regency and Radisson (403), Kenzi Tower (TLS error), % Arabica (418), PAUL Maroc (connection refused) and Musée Abderrahman Slaoui (domain did not resolve). Iloli publishes no address. No official sites were found for La Sqala, the Musée du Judaïsme Marocain, the Aïn Sebaâ zoo, Parc de la Ligue Arabe or several Corniche and city-centre restaurants, so the guide has no public park.
