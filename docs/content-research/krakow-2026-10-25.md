## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability, and seasonal or holiday hours may differ from the weekly hours quoted. Several sites could not be read because of certificate or SSL errors, 403 responses, DNS failures or redirects to social media (including Kraków Zoo, Massolit, Café Noworolski, Hamsa, Hotel Stary, Pochlebstwo, Zmączeni, Bottiglieria 1881 and Karma Coffee), so those places were left out rather than treated as closed. Opening hours were not published on the pages read for Starka, Szara Gęś, Pimiento (Stolarska), Alchemia and Wesoła Café, and Stary Kleparz gives days but not times. Wieliczka Salt Mine, Pod Aniołami and several restaurants were confirmed from current hours, menus or booking pages without an explicitly dated 2026 notice. The Wawel hours quoted apply to November 2026.
