## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several sites carry no 2026-dated content, so their hours are attributed to the website rather than stated as current: Lorenzillo's, La Habichuela, Ilios, Harry's, Garrafón, Señor Frog's (2023 group menu) and Café Antoinette (undated pages). Harry's lists hours that appear to be an error, and Fantino, KAYANTÁ Spa, Café de la Playa and Bar A-Kan publish no regular hours; NIZUC does not say whether non-guests can book its restaurants. RosaNegra's page could not be read, Ultramar's ferry sites refused or reset the connection, Xailing returned 403, and the Bisquets Obregón branch list did not load; none of these failures is evidence of closure. Ventura Park says it is closed indefinitely and The Club Grill is paused. Most named cafés, bakeries and taquerías in the original list had no official website.
