## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Skyline Queenstown and Coronet Peak returned 403 errors, the QLDC site (Queenstown Gardens) showed a bot check, Rātā and Madam Woo had certificate errors, The Boatshed returned 401, Bardeaux refused the connection, and Remarkables Market, Arrowtown Bakery, Ivy & Lola's, Wolf and Odyssey did not resolve; none of these failures is evidence of closure. Hours were not published on the pages read for Little Blackwood, Blue Kanu, Sherwood, the TSS Earnslaw and Walter Peak. Hours for Yonder and The Winery come from undated pages, and the Cookie Bar page is copyright-dated 2025. Bespoke Kitchen and the Kawarau Bungy Centre show conflicting hours on their own pages. Kiwi Park and the Kawarau Bungy Centre do not give exact dates for their summer and winter hours beyond what is quoted.
