## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. The Citadel, Roman Theater and Jerash could not be confirmed: the Department of Antiquities and Ministry of Tourism sites refused connection, and the Jordan Pass hours page is dated 2022 with no address. The Jordan Museum's site is being redeveloped. The Royal Automobile Museum, Royal Tank Museum, Four Seasons, W Amman, Grand Hyatt, Le Royal, Books@Cafe, Mijana, Al Balad Theater and Nabad Gallery could not be read (connection, DNS, SSL or 403/423 errors). Opening hours are not published for Habibah Sweets, Shams El Balad, Dimitri's Coffee branches, Bar on Four, Threesixty or the Royal Film Commission; Fairmont venues show only promotion times. Fakhreldin's hours come from a page dated 2025, and The Ritz-Carlton, Amman dining page carries no date. Habibah Sweets branches are named by street only, without building numbers.
