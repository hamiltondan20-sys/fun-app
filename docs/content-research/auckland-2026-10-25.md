## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Sky Tower and Fullers360 ferry pages sent automated readers to a bot check, and La Cigale French Market, Man O' War and Hello Beautiful had certificate errors, so none of them could be read. Daily Bread, Kōkako, Eighthirty, Espresso Workshop and Devonport Chocolates returned rate-limit errors, and The Grove's site failed to load. SEA LIFE Kelly Tarlton's published no street address, and Auckland Council pages (Wintergardens, Arataki) returned HTTP 406. Auckland War Memorial Museum's regular weekly hours and Mudbrick's restaurant hours were not shown on the pages read, and Prego publishes "Open 7 Days" without times. Coffee coverage is thin because most specialty café sites could not be read.
