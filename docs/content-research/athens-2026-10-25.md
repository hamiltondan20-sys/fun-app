## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. The Acropolis archaeological site could not be confirmed because the Ministry of Culture page refused connection and hhticket.gr and culture.gov.gr returned 403; the Benaki Museum, SNFCC and the Hilton (Galaxy Bar) also returned 403, Kuzina and the Eugenides Planetarium had certificate errors, and Strofi returned HTTP 520. Several input places (Kora Bakery, Takis Bakery, Dope Roasting Co., Tailor Made, Nolan, This is Loco, Betty's Bakery, Kostas Souvlaki) had no readable official site, and the domains formerly used by Nolan and Six d.o.g.s now show gambling content. Opening hours were not published on the pages read for the Acropolis Museum, Goulandris, Spondi, Karamanlidika, Hytra, Feyrouz, Lukumades, Mokka, Little Kook, The Clumsies, Line, Brettos and A for Athens; Lalaounis hours were only partly readable. For Lukumades and Brettos, current operation rests only on a 2026 copyright notice, and Line and A for Athens on booking links without 2026 content.
