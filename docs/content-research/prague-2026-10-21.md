## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Prague Castle's official site (hrad.cz) refused automated reading with HTTP 403, so the castle is not included; that is not evidence of closure. Black Angel's Bar could not be read (connection reset), and U Fleků's contact page returned 404, so both are left out. Petřín Lookout Tower and the Old Town Bridge Tower are not listed on the Museum of Prague's own site, and tourism-board pages were excluded by method. Charles Bridge, Old Town Square and the Letná viewpoint are public spaces with no operator page giving operating evidence. Several places (Café Louvre, Café Savoy, Café Slavia, Kantýna, La Degustation Bohême Bourgeoise, Field, Hemingway Bar, Můj šálek kávy, both Eska branches and the House at the Golden Ring) showed current hours or booking but no 2026-dated content, and the Mucha Museum, U Zlatého tygra and Anonymous Bar carried only a 2026 copyright line. Prague Zoo's monthly hours and news gave no year. Vyšehrad and the Strahov Monastery published only partial hours, and Municipal House tour hours and Rudolfinum box-office hours were not on the pages read.
