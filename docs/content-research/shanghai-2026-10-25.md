## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Many Shanghai operators publish mainly through WeChat or mini-programs, and a large number of official sites could not be read by automated fetch (connection refused, HTTP 403/405/418, certificate or SSL errors, or empty pages), including Yu Garden, the Oriental Pearl Tower, Shanghai Tower, the Long Museum, Power Station of Art, Lost Heaven, Seesaw Coffee and several luxury hotels; none of these failures is evidence of closure. No independent bakery or coffee roaster could be confirmed, so the bakery and coffee lists rely on hotel cafés. Hours were not published for the Shanghai Zoo, the Fairmont Peace Hotel's Jazz Bar, Cathay Room, Victor's Café and Peace Museum, or the Shanghai World Financial Center observatory. Rockbund Art Museum, the CPC memorial, the Shanghai Jewish Refugees Museum and Polux by Paul Pairet showed current hours but no dated 2026 content, and Cachet's weekday closing time is printed ambiguously as '00:00 am'.
