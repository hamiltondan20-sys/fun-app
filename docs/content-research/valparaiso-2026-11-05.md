## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several restaurant sites carry no 2026 date (Restaurant La Concepción is undated, Portofino's footer reads 2025, Tres Peces' 2024, MarAlegre's latest content is 2025, TallerCafé is undated), so their hours are attributed to those pages rather than stated as current. Hours were not published for Teatro Municipal, Biblioteca Santiago Severín, the Museo del Inmigrante, MarAlegre, Hotel Brighton, María María, Stefani and the Jardín Cervecero. The Destino Valparaíso tenants are confirmed through the complex's own site, not individual websites. Café Turri (connection reset), MUGO (certificate error), the Congreso Nacional (403 on camara.cl and senado.cl) and several restaurant domains that did not resolve could not be read; these failures are not evidence of closure. No official operator page with current funicular (ascensor) hours or fares was found.
