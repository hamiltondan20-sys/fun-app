## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. The Fine Arts Department's site pages for individual temples publish no opening hours. The department's older service page gives foreign fees of 50 baht single and 220 baht combined, which differ from the 2026 notice used here (80 and 300 baht). Chao Sam Phraya museum hours and its Monday closure come from a December 2022 notice and were not restated on a 2026 page. Chantharakasem museum hours were not published on the pages read. Sala Ayutthaya hours come from pages marked 2024. Baan Pomphet, Elephantstay and both Krungsri River Hotel outlets had no dated 2026 text, and the Krungsri River outlets publish no hours. The Japanese Village's new admission fees are only in an image. Baan Hollanda returned nothing readable, Kantary's hotel site returned 403, Classic Kameo's domain did not resolve, Baan Thai House had a certificate error, and the Starbucks Thailand store locator returned 403. No official sites were found for Bang Pa-In Palace, Wat Phanan Choeng, Wat Yai Chai Mongkhon or several well-known local restaurants, so they are omitted. Only 21 places could be confirmed, below the 25 to 35 target.
