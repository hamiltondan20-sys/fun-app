## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. The input guide contained only generic entries, so every place here was added and checked separately. Several confirmed places publish hours only on undated or older pages: Museo Amano (copyright 2021), Museo Oro del Perú (2018), Restaurante Huaca Pucllana (2020), Ayahuasca Restobar (2023), El Chinito (2025) and Pastelería San Antonio (latest dates 2024–2025); Hotel B, Cosme and Larcomar pages are undated, and Larcomar, Rafael, El Mercado, El Chinito and Belmond Miraflores Park publish no regular hours. MALI lists exhibitions by single dates, not ranges, so run lengths are unclear. Kjolle's booking page says "Reservas disponibles hasta finales de Octubre", so it is left out of the guide lists until the position after October is clear. Sites that could not be read include MATE (connection refused), Circuito Mágico del Agua (TLS error), Parque de las Leyendas (HTTP 418), Museo Central (bot check), Casa de la Literatura and Country Club Lima Hotel (HTTP 403), Puku Puku and La Rosa Náutica (HTTP 429), and Gran Hotel Bolívar (SSL error). Casa de Aliaga's site carries unrelated gambling links, and the Museo Andrés del Castillo and Convento de San Francisco catacomb domains redirect to unrelated sites. Astrid y Gastón, Mayta, Panchita, Tanta and ChocoMuseo publish no usable address on the pages read.
