## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. The Temple of Heaven, Summer Palace, Beihai Park and Beijing Zoo were confirmed through the Beijing Municipal Administration Center of Parks, which gives management-office addresses and recent 2026 activity notices but not public opening hours; their own sites could not be read (connection refused or certificate errors). The Palace Museum publishes its entrance gate but no street address on the pages read, and its English site returned HTTP 502. Mutianyu Great Wall and the National Stadium are clearly operating but published no address on the pages read, so they are not used. Restaurant hours were not published for Quanjude, Bianyifang or Donglaishun, and their operating evidence is group-level 2026 content. Universal Beijing Resort's park schedule page returned no readable hours. Butterfly Patisserie's only dated marker is a 2026 page footer. Several hotel and café sites (Peninsula, Bulgari, Four Seasons, Park Hyatt, Shangri-La, Lama Temple, % Arabica) blocked automated reading, and TRB Hutong and Da Dong had certificate errors, so there are few confirmed coffee, bakery and cocktail options. Capital Museum's hours are described as provisional ("暂定").
