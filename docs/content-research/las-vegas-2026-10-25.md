## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Every MGM Resorts property page (Bellagio, ARIA, Mandalay Bay, Park MGM and The Cosmopolitan venues) could not be fetched, so the Bellagio fountains, Bardot Brasserie, Shark Reef Aquarium, Best Friend, The Chandelier and Secret Pizza are left out; Freed's Bakery, Vesta Coffee and Mothership Coffee returned rate-limit errors, PublicUs had a certificate error, Golden Steer's site failed to load, and The LINQ's High Roller page had no readable content. None of these failures is evidence of closure. Hours were not published on the pages read for Springs Preserve, Esther's Kitchen, Bouchon (The Venetian) and Sphere; Lotus of Siam's hours were inconsistent; District Deli & Coffee showed only the current day's hours; and the Dominique Ansel shops are listed by resort without a street address. Several confirmed bars, cafés and restaurants showed current hours or booking but no 2026-dated content.
