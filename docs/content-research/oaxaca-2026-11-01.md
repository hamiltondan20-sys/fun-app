## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several hours come from pages that are undated (INAH Lugares pages, Origen, Pitiona) or carry older footers (Fundación Alfredo Harp Helú venue pages © 2022, Chocolate Mayordomo © 2021, Jacobo y María Ángeles with 2025 items), so treat them as published rather than current. INAH's Museo de las Culturas page lists Tuesday to Saturday hours yet also mentions free Sunday entry for Mexican citizens. Pitiona's Spanish and English hours differ for Saturday, and MUFI's own site gives Constitución 201 while its operator lists Reforma 504. Hours were not published for the Museo Textil de Oaxaca, El Asador Vasco, Quinta Real Oaxaca's venues, Pabilo Café or Selva. The Monte Albán zone page returned 404, so Monte Albán rests on the INAH site museum page. Sites that could not be read: Mezcaloteca, Café Brújula and Mezcal Real Minero (HTTP 429), El Destilado and Cafebre (403), In Situ Mezcalería (expired certificate), Sabina Sabe (TLS error), Expendio Tradición (connection closed), Rey Campero (522), Boulenc and Pan:AM (no working site), and the Oaxaca state culture site (500). Bakery coverage is thin: only Pabilo Café, which mentions house-baked bread, was confirmed.
