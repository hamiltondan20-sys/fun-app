## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Only 21 places met the address rule, below the 25-35 target. Ten major sights, including the Grand Egyptian Museum, the Egyptian Museum, the Citadel and the Cairo Opera House, were left out because their official pages publish no address or location, not because of any closure. The Pyramids of Giza, Saqqara and Al-Moez Street are confirmed by site name only, the ministry's seasonal date ranges appear only in images, Kempinski Nile Hotel pages give only "Garden City Cairo", and Khufu's publishes no opening hours. Several candidates could not be read: gem.eg, egyptianmuseumcairo.eg, Four Seasons, Diwan and Espresso Lab returned 403; Al-Azhar Park and Felfela had SSL errors; Abou El Sid, Cairo Jazz Club and Cairo Tower returned no readable content; the Nile Ritz-Carlton returned 404 and Marriott pages were then rate-limited.
