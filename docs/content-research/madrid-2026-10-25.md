## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. The Prado, CaixaForum Madrid and Confitería El Riojano returned HTTP 403, Hola Coffee returned HTTP 429, and Toma Café, Misión Café, 1862 Dry Bar, Casa Labra, Angelita, Moulin Chocolat, Panod, Panic, the Teleférico and the Museo Nacional de Ciencias Naturales failed on connection, certificate, DNS or server errors, so they are not included; none of these failures is evidence of closure. Sobrino de Botín's homepage was too large to read. Several confirmed places were published only partially: DiverXO and Corral de la Morería give no full weekly hours, Café Comercial publishes no hours, the Reina Sofía hours were not clearly labelled, and Chocolatería San Ginés gives inconsistent statements about 24-hour opening. Casa Lucio, Lhardy, Casa Alberto, Mercado de San Antón, Café de Oriente, Casa Dani and La Mallorquina showed current hours but little or no dated 2026 content, so they are rated medium confidence.
