## Limitations

This is a website review, not a visit or a telephone check, and advertised opening hours, tickets and menus don't guarantee availability. Only 10 places could be confirmed, below the 25–35 target, and no bakery or coffee place could be confirmed, so those lists are empty. Hours and prices for the antiquities come from the Ministry's online ticketing site, egymonuments.com, which carries no dates. Qubbat Al-Hawa Cemetery's hours come from a Ministry page with a 2019 copyright footer. Basma Hotel publishes no hours for its restaurants and bars. The Old Cataract (now run by Mandarin Oriental) and Mövenpick Resort Aswan couldn't be read on their official sites. Pyramisa Isis Island publishes its restaurants and hours but no exact location. The Nubia Museum, the Unfinished Obelisk, St Simeon's Monastery, Sehel Island and Kom Ombo have Ministry pages but no published location beyond "Aswan". Tolip, Helnan, Sonesta, Marhaba Palace and Cilantro sites failed to load or returned 404. Anakato's site carries injected spam and wasn't used.
