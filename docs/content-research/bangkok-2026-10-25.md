## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several input places could not be checked: Wat Arun, Jay Fai, Here Hai, Som Tam Nua, Teens of Thailand and Conkey's had no official website identified. Gaggan Anand, Kay's Boutique Breakfast, Landhaus Bakery and Eric Kayser's guessed domains did not resolve. Rocket Coffeebar, Factory Coffee and Nana Coffee Roasters failed or rate-limited, and the Bamboo Bar page showed only navigation. Tropic City's own site says it has closed. BACC, the Siam Society, The Peninsula, Banyan Tree, Four Seasons and Capella returned 403. Some places are published only partially: Bangkok National Museum, ICONSIAM, One Bangkok, Sorn, Le Du, Mezzaluna and Sky Bar had no readable weekly hours. nahm and SEA LIFE gave a building, not a street address. The Grand Palace, Le Du, Vesper and Asiatique pages carried no 2026-dated content.
