## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several sites could not be read: the Calanques National Park site (DNS failure), Notre-Dame de la Garde (the .fr domain now shows unrelated casino content and the .com refused the connection), L'Épuisette, Les Halles de la Major (expired certificate), La Caravelle (503), Les Bords de Mer, Abbaye Saint-Victor (403), Deep Coffee Roasters (429) and Carry Nation (error page); the domains listed for Risette and Café Coogee no longer belong to the cafés. Le Petit Nice, the Capian bar and the Dantès Skylounge publish no hours, Le Four des Navettes publishes no hours, and Sépia, Julis, AM par Alexandre Mazzia and Le Four des Navettes show no dated 2026 content beyond live booking or shop pages. The Mucem pages read give entrance locations but no street number. Cosquer Méditerranée labels its published hours as 2024. Torréfaction Noailles' Canebière page does not say whether coffee is served on site. Notices that end within two weeks, such as the city museums' 1 November closure, Frac Sud's strike-weekend closure and Mama Shelter's Halloween events, were left out of the guide copy. No official page was found for a food market, so markets are not covered.
