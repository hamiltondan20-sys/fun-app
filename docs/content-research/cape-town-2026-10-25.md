## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. SANParks pages (Cape of Good Hope, Boulders) returned 403 and the V&A Waterfront site and several coffee roasters (Truth, Bootlegger, Espresso Lab, Origin, Rosetta, Tribe, Deluxe) returned 429, so they are not included; Cape Point gives no exact address and says its funicular closes for maintenance from 26 October 2026. Kirstenbosch and Groot Constantia did not publish a street address on the pages read. Iziko pages give inconsistent winter hours and no exhibition run dates; Iziko, District Six Museum, Hemelhuijs and Knead (Muizenberg) showed no 2026-dated content; La Colombe and Harbour House (Kalk Bay) publish no street number; Neighbourgoods Market and Planet Bar publish no hours. Coffee entries are cafés and bakeries rather than specialist roasters because roaster sites could not be read.
