## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Taikoo Li shops and restaurants were confirmed only through the mall operator's online directory, whose store pages give unit numbers and hours but little or no description and carry no dates of their own, so their hours are attributed to that directory; Niccolo Chengdu's dining pages show posted hours but no dated 2026 content except The Tea Lounge's 2026 menu. Upper House Chengdu (formerly The Temple House) books its restaurants by email. Wuhou Shrine Museum publishes no weekly closing day. The Sichuan Museum site returned only its title, the Du Fu Thatched Cottage site refused connection, the Chengdu IFS site timed out, and Shangri-La Chengdu blocked automated reading (HTTP 403). People's Park, Kuanzhai Alley, Wenshu Monastery and several other sights have no official website we could find. The Jinsha Site Museum is closed until 30 April 2027 according to its own site.
