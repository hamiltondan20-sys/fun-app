## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and ticket details do not guarantee availability. HRH Hotels' pages (City Palace Museum, Crystal Gallery, car collection, Shiv Niwas, Garden Hotel and Jagmandir venues) give no street addresses, only named palace buildings, and list no closing days; HRH's published boat fares were valid only to 31 March 2026, so current fares were not confirmed, and boat hours are given only for undefined summer and winter seasons. The City Palace Museum's own site carries a 2025 footer and its e-ticket site failed with a certificate error. Raffles Udaipur's dining pages are undated; Ambrai's site is dated 2025 and publishes no hours; Palki Khana, the car collection and Bagore Ki Haveli publish no hours. The Leela's bar and Dining Room menus are labelled 2023. Sites that could not be read: Taj Lake Palace (HTTP 403), Hotel Lake Pichola/Upre (connection reset), Rajasthan museums department (DNS failure). Jagdish Temple, Sajjangarh/Monsoon Palace, Jheel's, Millets of Mewar and Bharatiya Lok Kala Mandal had no official site found. The Mewar Sound and Light Show is suspended till further notice. Two WebFetch reports of AI-addressed text (Leela home page, Garden Hotel contact page) only echoed the review prompt and were not reproduced on re-read, so they are not listed.
