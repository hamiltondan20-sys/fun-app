## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and show details do not guarantee availability. Monument hours are taken from the ministry's ticket site (egymonuments.com), which carries no date; the ministry's information pages on egymonuments.gov.eg (footer dated 2019) list different closing times, for example Karnak to 05:00 PM and Luxor Temple to 08:00 PM, so check at the ticket office. Monument locations are published only as descriptions (east or west bank, distance from Karnak), not street addresses. The Al Moudira venues publish no hours, Al-Sahaby Lane publishes only breakfast hours, Sindbad Balloons does not name its launch site, and the Nile Valley Hotel gives a location rather than a street address. Sofitel Winter Palace (Accor pages gone or redirected), Hilton Luxor (403), Sofra Restaurant & Café and Hod Hod Soliman (domains did not resolve) could not be read; the Luxor Museum, Mummification Museum, Ramesseum and Medinet Habu were left out because no official page read gives their exact location. No dedicated bakery or coffee shop with an official website was found; those lists rely on hotel restaurant menus.
