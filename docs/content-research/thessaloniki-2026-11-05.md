## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several official sites could not be read: modiano.gr, zythos.gr and tsarouhas.gr returned certificate errors; ouzoumelathron.gr, kitchenbar.gr and mourga.gr did not resolve; charoupi.gr, pemf.gr and daiosluxuryliving.gr failed DNS lookups; valenio.gr refused the connection; odysseus.culture.gr refused the connection; makedoniapalace.com redirects to plain http. Published only partially: Thessaloniki Concert Hall and Teloglion box-office or opening hours were not readable; the Museum of the Macedonian Struggle showed only a same-day closing banner; Agora Modiano and Fellows Bar publish no hours; the Rotunda and Heptapyrgion evidence is the current Hellenic Heritage listing with no dated 2026 notice; Coffee Island, Mikel Coffee, Terkenlis, Tzeneral and Balboa are confirmed from current store lists or hours rather than dated news, and Balboa's street number differs between 42 and 44 Pavlou Mela on the same page. The Museum of Byzantine Culture's own visit page lists Tuesday closure for the whole winter, while its homepage says it is open daily through November; the guide follows the homepage. The Jewish Museum of Thessaloniki was left out for lack of dated current content.
