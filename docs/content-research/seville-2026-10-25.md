## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several sites could not be read: Plaza de España's city operator (sevilla.org, connection refused), Museo de Bellas Artes (certificate error), CaixaForum (403), Torre del Oro and Isla Mágica (domains did not resolve), Hotel Doña María and Hotel Alfonso XIII (certificate errors) and Lonja del Barranco (connection refused). Hours were not published or readable for Cañabota, El Rinconcillo, Confitería Ochoa, La Terraza del EME, Hotel Inglaterra's terrace, the Archivo General de Indias and Casa de la Memoria; the Macarena basilica publishes only a 2023-24 winter schedule; Abantal's published hours may be phone hours; and Setas de Sevilla, Palacio de Lebrija, Museo del Baile Flamenco, abba The Roof Giralda, Cervecería Giralda and Confitería Ochoa show no explicitly 2026-dated content. No specialty coffee shop or independent bakery with a readable official site was confirmed, so the coffee and bakery lists rely on one confitería.
