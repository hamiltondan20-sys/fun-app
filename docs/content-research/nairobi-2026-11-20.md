## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. The Kenya Wildlife Service site (Nairobi National Park) failed with a certificate error, and Artcaffe, Connect Coffee, Ankole, Lord Erroll and the Kenya National Archives also failed on certificate or SSL errors; Kazuri Beads reset the connection, the Park Inn Attic rooftop page returned 403, and several restaurant domains did not resolve or were parked. Karen Blixen Museum, Giraffe Centre, Carnivore, Fairmont The Norfolk and several malls publish no exact street address, so they were left out. Hours are not published for Graze, LUCCA, the Balcony Bar, K Lounge, Spring Valley Coffee, Pallet Café, Nairobi Street Kitchen or the Kenya Cultural Centre; NMK hours come from its general rates page rather than each site's own page; Mandhari's hours come from a Serena page with a 2022 footer and Le Grenier à Pain's from an undated branch list. The Talisman, Le Grenier à Pain and Mandhari pages show no dated 2026 content. Cultiva's reservations page contained unrelated slot-game text, suggesting the site may be compromised. Two WebFetch summaries (Sankara homepage, Fairmont page) reported text addressed to an AI, but a second read of Sankara found none and neither report could be quoted, so both are treated as false flags.
