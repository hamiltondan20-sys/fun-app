## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several sites could not be read: Jorvik Viking Centre and Barley Hall returned 403, Treasurer's House showed a bot check, Bettys and Merchant Adventurers' Hall returned empty pages, and several smaller cafés and bars failed on DNS, certificate or connection errors; none of these failures is evidence of closure. Weekly hours were not published on the pages read for Middlethorpe Hall, The Grand, York and Ate O'Clock, and the City Walls opening-times page returned 404. Spring Espresso, Bluebird Bakery and Partisan showed current hours and menus but no dated 2026 content. The National Railway Museum is mid-redevelopment, so gallery access may change.
