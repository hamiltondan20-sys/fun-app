## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several civic museum homepages showed only that day's hours, not a weekly schedule. Basilica di San Marco hours sit on an external ticketing site that was not read. Caffè Florian, Al Covo, Trattoria alla Madonna and Experimental Cocktail Club Venice had no 2026-dated item and are rated medium confidence. Fondazione Querini Stampalia says its new website is still under construction. No dedicated bakery could be confirmed, so the bakery list uses Caffè Florian's pastry menu. These sites could not be read: Palazzo Grassi, Fondazione Cini, Torrefazione Cannaregio, Caffè Lavena and Ristorante Local (403 or fetch error), Ristorante Quadri and Majer (429), the Basilica dei Frari visit pages (404 or login screen), Il Mercante (expired certificate) and the City of Venice Rialto Market page (empty).
