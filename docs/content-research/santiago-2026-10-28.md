## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several official sites could not be read: Parque Metropolitano (Cerro San Cristóbal) refused connections, Mercado Central, La Vega Central, Viña Santa Rita, Mestizo, Mulato and others failed DNS lookup, Tren al Sur had a certificate error, MAC returned 403 and Cousiño Macul returned 429; these are not evidence of closure. Museo Histórico Nacional's own site says it is temporarily closed for expansion. Published information was partial for some places: Museo Chileno de Arte Precolombino's general weekly hours were on a tab not readable, Bocanáriz and Terraza Magnolia publish no hours, Emporio La Rosa (Merced) lists no Saturday hours, and La Chascona's page gives a March to December schedule without listing ticket prices. Several restaurant and café entries (Liguria, Galindo, Ambrosía Bistro, Castaño, Tavelli, Fuente Mardoqueo) rest on live booking links, published hours or a 2026 copyright footer rather than dated announcements.
