## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. The civic-museum site (museicivici.comune.verona.it) refused connection, so Castelvecchio, Casa di Giulietta and the Teatro Romano could not be checked; Rivamancina had a certificate error, Trattoria Tre Marchetti returned 403, and Garage Coffee Bros and Gli Ormesini returned 429. Hours were not published on the pages read for Torre dei Lamberti, Antica Bottega del Vino, Trattoria Al Pompiere, Locanda 4 Cuochi and Flego; several places (Giardino Giusti, Romeo Cocktail Bar, Terrazza Bar al Ponte, Caffè Coloniale, De Rossi) show hours without a date, and the funicular and Giardino Giusti do not give season change dates. The Arena box-office hours differ slightly between two Fondazione Arena pages.
