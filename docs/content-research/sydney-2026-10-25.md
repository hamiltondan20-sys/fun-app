## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several sites could not be read: BridgeClimb, Hyde Park Barracks and the Waverley Council coastal-walk page (HTTP 403), Cantina OK!, The Baxter Inn and Edition Coffee Roasters (certificate errors), Reuben Hills and Mecca Coffee (rate-limited), and Taronga Zoo, MCA, the State Library, Mr Wong and Totti's (no readable content); none of these failures indicates closure. SEA LIFE Sydney, Sydney Tower Eye, PS40, Paramount Coffee Project and The Rocks published no street address. Hours were not published for the Opera House, Rockpool, BridgeMuseum and Single O, Sydney Observatory gave days but no times, and Skittle Lane's King Street listing omits Thursday. Several venues (Saint Peter, Aria, Maybe Sammy, Bourke Street Bakery) showed current hours or booking pages but no dated 2026 text.
