## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several confirmed places show only light current evidence, such as a 2026 copyright line, a dated menu file or a promotion image (Seniman Coffee, Monsieur Spoon, Milk & Madu, KAFE, Kebun Bistro, Alas Harum Bali, Apéritif, Pinstripe Bar, Hanging Gardens of Bali, Threads of Life, Sayuri Healing Food); they are marked medium confidence. Hours were not published for The Yoga Barn, Pinstripe Bar, Bebek Bengil, Murni's Warung, Hanging Gardens of Bali or Tanah Gajah's restaurant; Komaneka Fine Art Gallery publishes two conflicting sets of hours and no exhibition run dates; Murni's Warung gives a location description rather than a street address, and its Christmas booking page carries an older post date. Sites that could not be read include Museum Puri Lukisan and Room4Dessert (certificate errors), Locavore NXT (SSL failure), COMO Uma Ubud, Four Seasons Sayan, The Samaya, Capella Ubud, Cretya Ubud and Bali Buda (403), Ubud Traditional Spa (429) and Museum Rudana (connection reset). Tegallalang Rice Terraces and Saraswati Temple have no official operator page, and Bridges Bali states it is closed permanently.
