## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. The Cité du Vin site failed with a certificate error and Le Gabriel refused the connection, so neither was checked; the Tour Pey-Berland is closed until further notice per its own site. Weekly hours were not published on the pages read for the MADD, Cap Sciences (today's hours only), Le Pressoir d'Argent, Le Rooftop and the Grand-Théâtre, whose address came from an older page on its own site. La Tupina, L'Entrecôte, La Pelle Café, Baillardran and La Toque Cuivrée showed current hours but no 2026-dated content. Symbiose's restaurant service is marked "Reprise indéterminée"; only its cocktail bar is listed.
