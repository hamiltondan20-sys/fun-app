## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability, and short-term notices (such as single-day closures, weekly show listings and seasonal food festivals) were left out of the guide copy. Several well-known places could not be read and are excluded: Museo Nacional de Antropología (HTTP 468), Palacio de Bellas Artes and Fifty Mils (HTTP 403), Pujol, Buna and Almanegra (HTTP 429), Chapultepec Park, Chapultepec Zoo and Museo del Estanquillo (connection refused), and Castillo de Chapultepec, Anahuacalli, Museo Jumex and Biblioteca Vasconcelos (DNS failures). Some confirmed places publish only partial details: Rosetta and Cafebrería El Péndulo show no opening hours, Basílica de Guadalupe's visiting hours were on a separate page not read, Museo Tamayo and MIDE do not list weekly opening days, Papalote's hours are not explicitly labelled as museum hours, Templo Mayor's current exhibition has no stated dates, and Bar La Ópera's hours are written in ambiguous shorthand.
