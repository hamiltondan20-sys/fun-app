## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several official sites could not be read: Museu Nacional de Machado de Castro (connection closed), Santa Clara-a-Velha (culturacentro.gov.pt DNS failure), Conímbriga (connection refused), À Capella (connection reset), Convento São Francisco, cm-coimbra.pt, uc.pt/jardimbotanico, uc.pt/museudaciencia, NH Hotels and bellaciao.pt (403), Dux (certificate error) and Basófias (server error); these are not evidence of closure. Mercado Municipal D. Pedro V, Torre de Almedina, Edifício Chiado and Casa-Museu Miguel Torga have current council news but no published address. Many well-known Baixa restaurants, pastry shops and cafés (Solar do Bacalhau, Zé Manel dos Ossos, Pastelaria Briosa, NAU Coffee) have no official website, so no bakery other than Café Santa Cruz could be confirmed. Hours are partial: no times are published for the Casa-Museu, Arcadas, Pedro e Inês, the Hotel Oslo rooftop bar, TAGV's box office or the Quinta das Lágrimas gardens. Portugal dos Pequenitos' hours page is undated, Café Santa Cruz's and UC Exploratório's ticket pages date from 2023, and A Tasca das Tias Camellas' page carries no current date. The University's tourism page describes UC Exploratório as open daily, while UC Exploratório's own agenda lists it as closed on Mondays; this guide follows the venue's own agenda.
