## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Intramuros site hours (Fort Santiago, Casa Manila Museum, Baluarte de San Diego, Museo de Intramuros) come from an Intramuros Administration page marked as updated in September 2023, and Eng Bee Tin's hours come from a page with nothing newer than October 2025. Hours were not readable for the Ayala Museum, Rizal Park or Gallery by Chele, and Toyo Eatery, Run Rabbit Run, Gallery by Chele and the Raffles Makati outlets showed no 2026-dated content. National Museum hours for Natural History and Anthropology come from museum-wide notices. Sites that could not be read automatically (403, certificate or connection errors) include The Peninsula Manila, Okada Manila, Shangri-La The Fort, Wildflour, The Curator, the Cultural Center of the Philippines, San Agustin Church, Pancake House, Goldilocks, Polland, Toby's Estate and Kamuning Bakery.
