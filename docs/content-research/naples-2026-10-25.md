## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several major sites could not be read and are not included: the National Archaeological Museum (MANN), the Tesoro di San Gennaro, Sfogliatelle Attanasio and the Darwin-Dohrn Museum had certificate errors; Capodimonte and the Naples zoo refused connections; Teatro di San Carlo showed a bot check; and several bars, bakeries and pizzerias had domains that did not resolve. Hours were not published for Palazzo Reale, Città della Scienza, Mimì alla Ferrovia, Palazzo Petrucci, Il Malandrino, Gambrinus, Scaturchio and Mennella. Santa Chiara's September 2026 closure notice is an image whose dates could not be read. Several places, including Napoli Sotterranea, da Michele, Di Matteo, Pio Monte, Scaturchio and Mennella, showed no 2026-dated content and are marked medium confidence.
