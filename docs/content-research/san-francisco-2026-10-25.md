## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability, and several operators note that hours can change for holidays and events. Some confirmed places showed no 2026-dated content and are rated medium (Plow, Tadich Grill, House of Prime Rib, Buena Vista Cafe, b. patisserie, the Ritual Coffee branches, Philz Coffee); the NPS Alcatraz pages were last updated in 2021. Hours were not published on the pages read for Outerlands, Golden Gate Park or the cable cars, and Philz showed same-day hours only. The Golden Gate Bridge site gives no street address. These sites could not be read: de Young and Exploratorium (403), Blue Bottle (403), Andytown, Equator and Four Barrel (429), Tosca Cafe (certificate error), Musée Mécanique (connection refused), La Taqueria and Zazie (domain not found), Asian Art Museum visit page (404) and City Lights (redirect). Sightglass and Swan Oyster Depot domains redirected to unrelated sites. None of these failures is evidence of closure.
