## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several places publish hours but no dated 2026 content (Antigua Casa de Guardia, El Mesón de Cervantes, Casa Lola and Pez Lola, Santa Coffee, Museo Revello de Toro), and the cathedral's hours table carries no year. Kaleja, Chester & Punk and La Terraza at the Catalonia Molina Lario publish no opening hours; Palodú publishes only closing days; La Canasta gives hours only for its Avenida de la Aurora shop. Playa de la Malagueta has no street address, only coordinates on the city page. The Mercado Central de Atarazanas is left out because no official page could be found. The Museo de Málaga, CAC Málaga, Casa Aranda, La Recova, the Museo Automovilístico, Museo Alborania, Café Central, Los Mellizos and the Hotel Larios terrace could not be read because of certificate errors, 403/520 responses or timeouts. Those failures are not evidence of closure.
