## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. The Duomo, Museo Ferragamo and several hotel bar sites (Four Seasons, Lungarno Collection, Rocco Forte) returned HTTP 403; the City of Florence, Musei Civici Fiorentini and regional museums (Museo di San Marco) sites refused connections; and the Galleria dell'Accademia, Caffè Gilli, Trattoria Mario, St. Regis, Gelateria Santa Trinita, Badiani and Paszkowski sites had certificate or SSL errors. None of these failures is evidence of closure. Several food entries are confirmed only at medium confidence, from a live booking system, published hours or a 2026 copyright line: Harry's Bar Firenze, Osteria Santo Spirito and Ora d'Aria showed no 2026-dated content, and Vivoli, Ditta Artigianale, Pasticceria Buonamici, B-Roof, Trattoria Zà Zà and Enoteca Pinchiorri publish no opening hours. Santa Croce, Santa Maria Novella, San Miniato al Monte and Museo Galileo regular hours were not readable. Palazzo Pitti, the Boboli Gardens and Orsanmichele have no street number. Museo Novecento and Harry's Bar pages contradict themselves on opening days. The strike notice is attributed only to the Uffizi Galleries' notice page; the Pitti and Boboli pages carry only its headline.
