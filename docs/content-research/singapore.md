## Operator pages opened and read

- Gardens by the Bay: https://www.gardensbythebay.com.sg/en/plan-your-visit.html
  and https://www.gardensbythebay.com.sg/en/plan-your-visit/opening-hours.html
  list visitor access, tickets and outdoor areas at 18 Marina Gardens Drive.
  Flower Dome maintenance closure is listed for 6 October 2026. Check the
  calendar again before publication. Do not imply every conservatory is open
  or that paid attractions are included with outdoor access.
- Singapore Botanic Gardens:
  https://sbg.nparks.gov.sg/visit/general-info/ gives free main-garden entry,
  separately charged National Orchid Garden entry and entrance-specific MRT
  stations. Jacob Ballas Children's Garden admits children up to 14 with an
  accompanying adult and normally closes Mondays. Its page was updated on
  2 October 2026. A useful family candidate, not a solo-adult recommendation.
- National Gallery Singapore:
  https://www.nationalgallery.sg/sg/en/visit/visitor-information.html lists
  active admission and visitor services at 1 St. Andrew's Road. General
  admission is not universally free for overseas visitors. UOB Southeast Asia
  Gallery is closed until late 2027. Temporary October entrance changes also
  require checking; do not promise every gallery is available.
- ArtScience Museum: https://www.marinabaysands.com/museum.html lists active
  exhibition tickets and visitor hours at 6 Bayfront Avenue. Select an
  exhibition rather than implying one ticket includes every experience.
- Candlenut: https://www.comoepicurean.com/restaurants/candlenut/ lists
  Peranakan menus, lunch and dinner service and a booking link at 17A Dempsey
  Road. Suitable for a planned meal. No independent current corroboration
  recorded yet.
- National Kitchen by Violet Oon:
  https://violetoon.com/national-kitchen-by-violet-oon-national-gallery-singapore/
  lists menus and reservations at 1 St. Andrew's Road, #02-01, City Hall Wing.
  Lunch and dinner are listed daily; afternoon tea is not an everyday service.
- JUMBO Seafood (Riverside Point):
  https://www.jumboseafood.com.sg/en/riverside-point lists this exact branch at
  30 Merchant Road, #01-01/02, Singapore 058282, daily service and links to
  menus and reservations. Do not substitute another JUMBO branch.
- Hell's Museum at Haw Par Villa:
  https://www.hawparvilla.sg/hells-museum/ lists current ticket sales and
  multiple full-day October and November 2026 closures, despite a general
  daily-hours heading. Graphic afterlife displays need a suitability warning.
  Do not add to the children's list. Exact access address is on
  https://www.hawparvilla.sg/plan-your-visit/ (262 Pasir Panjang Road).
