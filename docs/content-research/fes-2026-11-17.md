## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. The Musée Al Batha page says both “Ouvert du lundi au dimanche” and “Fermé le mardi”; the guide follows the Tuesday closure. Opening hours were not published for L'Amandier, The Golden Bar, Amaraz, Restaurant La Maison Bleue, The Rooftop, the Palais Amani bar or Fez Cooking School, and Fez Café's and Karawan's restaurant pages returned 404, so their hours were not read. Café Clock and Dar Roumana showed no dated 2026 content, only current hours, prices and booking. The Musée Nejjarine site failed with a certificate error, a Riad Fès sub-page returned 403, and the Jnan Sbil PDF was too large to read. Bou Inania Madrasa, Al Quaraouiyine, the tanneries and Borj Nord had no official operator page with an address and hours, so they are left out. The Ruined Garden's and Nur's domains no longer show their restaurants. No bakery with an official page and Fès address was found, so the bakeries list is empty.
