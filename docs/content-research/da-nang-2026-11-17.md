## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several sites could not be read: Highlands Coffee, Starbucks, Hyatt and Hilton returned 403; Pizza 4P's had a certificate error; Bread of Life, Nén Danang and Naman Retreat did not resolve; Tous les Jours and Hải Sản Bé Mặn refused connections; the KATINAT store list did not load. Some confirmed places are published only partially: Marble Mountains, the InterContinental restaurants and bar, Olivia's Prime, Novotel and TIA give no opening hours; Da Nang Cathedral is confirmed only through the diocese site at the same address, without Mass times; Ba Hưng Bakery states one set of hours for all branches; the Pullman Infinity Bar's general hours were cut off; Furama's booking hours come from a restaurants page dated 30 May 2025; and Reaching Out's hours come from a location page that also carries an older Tết notice. Hội An places are now within Da Nang city, as the operators' own addresses show. Several fetch summaries reported AI-directed text that matched this review's own request wording, so it was treated as a tool artefact rather than page content.
