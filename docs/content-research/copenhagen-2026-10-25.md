## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. The input lists were entirely generic, so all 28 places were added as candidates. Some places were published only partially: Tivoli Gardens' homepage gave no opening hours, Mikkeller Bar Viktoriagade listed no hours, Designmuseum Danmark showed only today's hours, Rosenborg Castle's page showed hours only from 2 November, and Copenhagen Zoo's only for 25–31 October 2026. Noma's site says it returned to Copenhagen in August 2026 without an exact date. Kødbyens Fiskebar, Ruby, Geranium, Coffee Collective and Andersen Bakery showed hours or booking but no dated 2026 content. The Botanical Garden's Palm House is under renovation, with parts closed at times. Sites that could not be read: SMK (title only), Christiansborg Palace (placeholder), Den Blå Planet, Hija de Sanchez, Alouette and Copenhagen Coffee Lab (certificate errors), Atelier September (403), Hart Bageri (TLS error), La Cabra, Prolog and Apollo Bar (429 rate limits), and Duck and Cover (DNS failure).
