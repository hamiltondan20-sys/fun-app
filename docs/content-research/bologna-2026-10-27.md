## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several sites could not be read: MAMbo and Aroma (certificate errors), Museo Ducati and MAST (HTTP 403), the Museo Civico Archeologico (security check), and the Pinacoteca Nazionale (DNS failure and connection refused). The Two Towers' booking domain redirects to the tourism board, so it was not used. Hours were not published or not readable for Paolo Atti & Figli, Forno Brisa (Via Galliera), Salumeria Simoni, Ristorante I Portici and Café Marinetti, and only the day's hours were shown for the Archiginnasio and Salaborsa. Several restaurants (Tamburini, Osteria dell'Orsa, Diana, Al Pappagallo, Pasticceria Impero) publish hours but no dated 2026 content, so they are rated medium confidence.
