## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. The Hungarian Parliament site showed a CAPTCHA; the Museum of Fine Arts and Hungarian National Gallery returned HTTP 451; Ruszwurm, High Note SkyBar and Madal Café returned 403; Central Café, Boutiq'Bar, My Little Melbourne, Szimply, Pékműhely, Hopp, Művész Kávéház and Butter Brothers had certificate or TLS errors; several other domains did not resolve. None of these failures is evidence of closure. Matthias Church's weekly schedule was not readable (only one day's hours), Kollázs published no opening hours, the Hungarian State Opera's box office hours were not shown, and the House of Terror Museum's reopening notice gives no year. Espresso Embassy, Artizán, Auguszt and Kollázs showed current hours or booking but no dated 2026 content. Gellért Thermal Bath is closed, with reopening expected in 2028.
