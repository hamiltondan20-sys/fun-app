## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Museum hours and monthly closing days come from the heritage conservation centre's own pages, which date from 2011 to 2015; the Museum of Folk Culture and History and Culture museum pages give closing days but no times, and no heritage ticket prices were published on the pages read. Tra Que and Thanh Ha are located only at village level. Hours were not published for Hoi An Memories Land, the Japanese Covered Bridge, Hoi An Roastery (84 Tran Cao Van) or the Tra Que and Thanh Ha villages; the Mango Hoi An and Secret Garden sites carry no 2026-dated content. VinWonders Nam Hoi An posted an operational notice dated 19 October 2026 that could not be read in full, and the park is in Thang Binh, outside Hoi An. The bakery list is thin: no stand-alone Hoi An bakery with a readable official site was found, so it rests on Hoi An Roastery's homemade croissant and a 2023 operator article on L'Annam's breakfast croissants. Sites that could not be read: Ms Vy's group (Morning Glory, Cargo Club) and Four Seasons The Nam Hai and Anantara (HTTP 403), Hotel Royal Hoi An, Cocobox and Dingo Deli (domains did not resolve), and Jack Tran Tours (redirect loop). Lune Center Hoi An published no venue address.
