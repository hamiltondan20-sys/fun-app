## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability, and temples and shrines note that hours can change for ceremonies. Kofuku-ji and Horyu-ji (certificate errors), the Nara National Museum (HTTP 403), the Nara Prefectural Museum of Art (expired certificate), Kamameshi Shizuka (DNS failure) and the Irie Taikichi photography museum (no readable content) could not be read, so they are left out; a failed fetch is not evidence of closure. Hours were not published on the pages read for Toshodai-ji, Gango-ji, Nara Hotel Mikasa and Nara Hotel The Bar, and closing days were not stated for Rokumei Coffee's Nara shop or the Kasuga Taisha Treasure Hall. JW Marriott Hotel Nara hours come from an undated dining page, and individual venue pages had been removed. Isuien Garden's guide page has no dated notices. The Nara Deer Preservation Foundation's site lists two unrelated blog entries about link building, which may indicate spam on that site. Shin-Yakushiji, Naramachi Koshi-no-ie and several food shops published addresses but no 2026 content.
