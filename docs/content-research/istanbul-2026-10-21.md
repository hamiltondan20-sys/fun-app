## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several official sites could not be read automatically. Hagia Sophia (ayasofyacamii.gov.tr) and the Blue Mosque (sultanahmetcamii.org) failed with certificate errors, as did the Spice Bazaar, Mandabatmaz and Develi. Karaköy Güllüoğlu, Hafız Mustafa's online store and Saray Muhallebicisi returned HTTP 403. The Basilica Cistern, 360 Istanbul and Baylan dropped or refused the connection. None of these failures is evidence of closure. No official website was found for Van Kahvaltı Evi, Privato Cafe, Alexandra, Moretenders, Galata Simitçisi or Coffee Department. Several other candidates had official sites without a branch address or current content. Pera Palace's Orient Bar says it is not operating until the end of the summer season, with no date. Several confirmed places were published only partially. Pandeli, Neolokal, the Grand Bazaar and Kurukahveci Mehmet Efendi publish no opening hours. Istanbul Modern showed only that day's hours. Petra's Şişhane branch has no street address. Arkestra's published hours read "Thuesday - Saturday", so the first open day is unclear. Many confirmed places, including the National Palaces pages, showed no 2026-dated content, so their operating status rests on current booking, menu, price or hours information.
