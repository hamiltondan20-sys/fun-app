## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability, and several hotel and museum sites note that hours may change. Kuromon Market and Brooklyn Roasting Company returned TLS errors; Abeno Harukas 300 and Takamura returned HTTP 403; Tennoji Zoo returned HTTP 503; teamLab Botanical Garden Osaka and Universal Studios Japan returned only page titles; and Kaiyukan's hours calendar did not load, so these are not included. Osaka Castle Main Tower is included with medium confidence: its address and advance booking were readable, but its opening hours and any dated 2026 notice were not. Hours were not published on the pages read for Shitennoji and Sumiyoshi Taisha. The Osaka Science Museum's closing-day calendar did not load, Rikuro's closing days are not listed, and Mondial Kaffee 328 lists two different closing times for its Club Ace branch. The Museum of Oriental Ceramics and the Osaka Museum of Housing and Living are closed for works.
