## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several official sites could not be read and those places were left out: the Covered Market and Turf Tavern (certificate errors), Blackwell's, Graduate Oxford, The Alchemist, Kazbar and The Old Bookbinders (403), Jericho Coffee Traders (429), Gee's and the Old Parsonage (connection errors), Turl Street Kitchen, Society Café, The Bear Inn and The Store Hotel (domains did not resolve), and Freud (no readable content). Opening hours were not published on the pages read for Christ Church, the Botanic Garden, Cherwell Boathouse, The Missing Bean and Pierre Victoire, and Malmaison's bar hours were not shown. Raoul's, Oli's Thai and Pierre Victoire showed no dated 2026 content, and Arbequina's Cowley Road hours were inconsistent on its own page.
