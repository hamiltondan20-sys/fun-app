## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Haedong Yonggungsa (connection refused), Jagalchi Market (HTTP 520), Busan Museum and the Busan Facilities Corporation site for Busan Tower and Taejongdae (connection reset), Momos Coffee and Park Hyatt Busan (HTTP 403), and Busan X the Sky, Busan Cinema Center and Paradise Hotel Busan (no readable content) could not be read, so they are left out; none of this is evidence of closure. Gamcheon Culture Village showed no notices newer than 2022, and the National Maritime Museum's pages gave hours but no street address while its website is suspended from 26 October to 28 December 2026. Haeundae Beach has directions but no street address on its official page. Hours were not published for Haeundae Blue Line Park or the Signiel Busan, Lotte Hotel Busan and Grand Josun Busan outlets; Busan Air Cruise and SEA LIFE showed only that day's hours. F1963 tenants were confirmed through the complex's own site, and The Westin Josun Busan outlets through Marriott's hotel page, which carried no dated 2026 notices. The Busan Citizens Park History Museum is closed from 2 to 16 November 2026 to install a new exhibition.
