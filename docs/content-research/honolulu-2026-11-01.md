## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Pearl Harbor National Memorial's hours come from an NPS page last updated October 9, 2025, and several places (Leonard's Bakery, Liliha Bakery, Duke's Waikiki, Helena's Hawaiian Food, KCC Farmers' Market, Battleship Missouri Memorial, Island Vintage Coffee) show 2026 only as a footer or asset date, so their hours are as listed rather than confirmed current. Side Street Inn, Koko Head Cafe, Morning Glass Coffee, Bar Leather Apron, ʻIolani Palace and Waikīkī Aquarium publish no 2026-dated content. Liliha Bakery's page gives two conflicting sets of hours, Rainbow Drive-In publishes no regular hours, ʻIolani Palace gives no tour times, and the Waikīkī Aquarium is open during renovations. Hanauma Bay (no official address page found), Foster Botanical Garden (official page 404) and Ted's Bakery (SSL error) could not be confirmed; Kona Coffee Purveyors and Eggs 'n Things lacked hours or an address. State parks posted hurricane-recovery notices on 10/29/26 for other parks; check Diamond Head before you go.
