## Limitations

This is a website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several major sites could not be read automatically and were left out rather than bypassed: Burj Khalifa, At.mosphere, The Espresso Lab, Dubai Aquarium, Dubai Opera, SALT and Sky Views returned HTTP 403; the Dubai Culture museum sites (Etihad Museum, Al Shindagha) and Bageri Form and Arabian Tea House returned certificate or SSL errors; Atlantis Aquaventure timed out; Nightjar Coffee and Mirzam were rate-limited; Coca-Cola Arena sent a queue page. Museum of the Future says it is temporarily closed with no reopening date. Global Village, LEGOLAND Dubai, Souk Al Seef and The Dubai Mall published hours or seasons but no street address on the pages read. Some confirmed places are published only partly: Orfali Bros gives dinner days without times, Comptoir 102 shows no 2026-dated content, Jameel Arts Centre's hours conflict with its Tuesday closure, CÉ LA VI and Al Fanar give location descriptions rather than street addresses, and Alserkal Avenue lists entrances rather than a single address. No bakery could be confirmed, so the bakeries list is empty, and only one coffee roaster was confirmed. The total of 17 confirmed places is below the 25-35 target.
