## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Jaipur's state-run monuments (Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, Albert Hall Museum, Jantar Mantar, Nahargarh Fort) and Jawahar Kala Kendra could not be confirmed because their government websites failed DNS lookup or, for the obms-tourist.rajasthan.gov.in ticketing site, returned a certificate error; these failures are not evidence of closure. Taj (Rambagh Palace), Hilton and Jaipur Rugs returned HTTP 403, Blue Tokai and Bikanervala returned HTTP 429, and Bar Palladio, LMB, 1135 AD, Curious Life, The Johri, SUJAN Rajmahal and Raj Mandir could not be reached. Several confirmed places were published only partially: City Palace gives gate locations rather than a street address; Niros, Chokhi Dhani, Anokhi Café, Peshawri, Jal Mahal and the Fairmont Jaipur venues publish no exact hours; Anokhi Café's page gives the Anokhi Farm address; Theobroma (C Scheme) shows conflicting hours; ISKCON Jaipur's darshan times overlap on its page; Amrapali Museum's Sunday closure is inferred from Monday–Saturday hours. The Leela, Fairmont and Oberoi Rajvilas venues are outside the old city at Kukas and Goner Road. Only 22 places were confirmed, below the 25–35 target.
