## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and menu details do not guarantee availability. The national parks department pages carry a 2015 footer, so their welfare-shop hours and the Mu Ko Lanta seasonal closure are taken from older pages and no fees are published. Several confirmed resort venues publish only partial details: Dusit Thani Krabi Beach Resort lists hours as "Seasonal", Aonang Villa Resort and Railay Village Resort publish no venue hours, MIRA's page gives conflicting closing times, Lae Lay Grill's page is undated and also says "Always Open", and Nakamanda and Sand Sea restaurant pages are undated. Sites that could not be read: Banyan Tree Krabi, Avani Ao Nang Cliff, Holiday Inn Resort Krabi Ao Nang Beach, Starbucks Thailand and Krabi Town Municipality (403), King Climbers (expired certificate), Sofitel Krabi Phokeethra, Railay Princess, Aonang Cliff Beach Resort and Maharat Bakery (domain errors), Beyond Resort's Krabi bakery (522) and Aonang Villa's Kiang Le page (connection dropped). Rayavadee's other restaurants are listed as temporarily closed, and Centara Grand Krabi appears to be relaunching as Centara Reserve Krabi with stays offered from 1 March 2027. Popular areas such as Railay Beach, Ao Nang beach and the Four Islands trip have no single operator website and are not covered as places.
