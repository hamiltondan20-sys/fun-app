## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Only 17 places could be confirmed, below the 25–35 target: most Hangzhou restaurants, cafés and bakeries publish only through apps or WeChat, and many official sites failed automated reading (403 or 418 responses at several hotel groups and % Arabica, expired or invalid certificates at the Zhejiang Provincial Museum, Hangzhou Grand Theatre, Songcheng, Zhiweiguan and Fuchun Resort, refused connections at Lou Wai Lou, Waipojia, the West Lake State Guest House and Xiling Seal Art Society, and unreadable or garbled pages at the Zhejiang Art Museum, China Academy of Art Museum, Zhejiang Museum of Natural History and Xixi Wetland). The China National Silk Museum was readable but published no street address. Four Seasons venues were checked on the operator's press site because its main site returned 403. Weekly hours were not published for Lingyin Temple, the Tea Museum, Liangzhu Museum or any restaurant; Amanfayun's dining page gives service periods only and no dated 2026 item; the Rooftop Stars Bar hours come from an undated press story and the hotel's fact sheet calls the rooftop bar Stellaris. West Lake's location is given through the committee's address and descriptions rather than a single street address.
