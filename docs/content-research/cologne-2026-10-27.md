## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Museum Ludwig, the Römisch-Germanisches Museum interim site, Früh am Dom and the NS-Dokumentationszentrum did not publish full weekly hours on the pages read; taku's hours were stated only for its October 2026 anniversary menu, and the Hanse Stube's only for goose season (30 Oct-23 Dec 2026). Peters Brauhaus, Päffgen, Café Reichard and Café Konditorei Wahlen showed no 2026-dated item beyond hours, menus or copyright lines. The Kölner Seilbahn goes into winter break after 8 November apart from Advent weekends. KölnTriangle, Odysseum, Little Link, Suderman, Van Dyck, Heilandt, Hallmackenreuther and Monkey Bar Köln could not be read (connection, DNS, TLS, 403 or 429 errors), Le Moissonnier's address and ERNST Kaffeeröster's house numbers were not readable, and City of Cologne pages for the Flora and the weekly markets returned HTTP 410, so no market, park or dedicated coffee roaster is included.
