## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. The ASI pages for Humayun's Tomb, Qutub Minar and Red Fort were readable but gave no address, so those monuments are left out. Sites that could not be read: National Museum (certificate error), NGMA New Delhi (404/no content), Lotus Temple and Pradhanmantri Sangrahalaya (403), Gandhi Smriti (expired certificate), National Rail Museum (connection refused), Karim's (fetch failed), Blue Tokai (429), Taj Mahal New Delhi (403), The Big Chill Cafe and L'Opera (domains did not resolve). The nscdelhi.org domain no longer serves the National Science Centre. Indian Accent, Safari Lounge and Lodhi Bakery publish no opening hours; Rashtrapati Bhavan's hours come from its contact page, not individual circuit pages; Baoshuan's hours come from the hotel's dining list, which differs slightly from Dhilli's own page on weekend lunch times. Lodhi Garden opening times vary by season on the NDMC page.
