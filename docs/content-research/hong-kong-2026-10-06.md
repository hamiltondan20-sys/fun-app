## Scope and Required Reader Copy

M+ has both paid galleries and free public spaces. Its budget placement must
explain the free-space scope visibly; it must not promise a free general museum
ticket. PMQ tenant hours vary. The ferry recommendation means the ordinary
Central-Tsim Sha Tsui crossing, not a harbour tour sold separately.

Duddell's Central and its airport outlet are not interchangeable. Bakehouse
Wan Chai advertises dine-in breakfast; several other branches are takeaway only.
noc's Sun Arcade branch is different from its nearby Empire Centre location.
Its index shows a Closed status alongside daily hours, while the individual
branch page advertises operating hours. Recheck that transient status before
publication; this review does not treat it as a permanent-closure notice.

Lung King Heen has child-age rules that vary by day. ARGO has its own age and
dress requirements. Both use the Four Seasons directions page, which currently
notes IFC escalator maintenance and lift access. No fixed price, availability,
Michelin rating or unrestricted family access will be promised in guide copy.
