## Limitations

This is a website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several official sites could not be read. LX Factory's page returned only encoded image data. Dear Breakfast and The Mill failed with SSL errors, and Sea Me's site refused the connection. No official site could be found for Taberna da Rua das Flores, Heim Cafe or Fauna & Flora. Red Frog and Monkey Mash publish no street address on the pages read. Padaria São Roque's operator site shows no hours or current activity. Some places were only partly published. Pastéis de Belém gives no opening times, Copenhagen Coffee Lab (Madalena) lists no hours, and Hello Kristof gives conflicting hours. Torre de Belém shows two different morning opening times. The Oceanário gives no street number. Torre de Belém, Jerónimos, Pastéis de Belém, Manteigaria and Cinco Lounge show no 2026-dated content beyond published hours or tickets. Museu Nacional do Azulejo, Museu Nacional de Arte Antiga (until 21 November) and Elevador de Santa Justa are currently closed.
