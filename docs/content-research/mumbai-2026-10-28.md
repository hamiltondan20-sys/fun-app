## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. The input lists were almost entirely generic, so every confirmed place was added as a gap candidate. Several major sites could not be read: CSMVS (no readable content), Dr. Bhau Daji Lad Museum (access denied), NMACC (reCAPTCHA), The Taj Mahal Palace and Four Seasons outlets (HTTP 403), and The Bombay Canteen, Subko, Blue Tokai and Bombay Sweet Shop (HTTP 429). NCPA publishes a current events calendar but no postal address on the pages read, and the Nehru Planetarium page carries an ambiguous 'remains closed in the year 2026' notice, so both were left out. Jehangir Art Gallery and DAG publish no opening hours; Jehangir shows two slightly different addresses. Oberoi outlets give only 'Nariman Point, Mumbai 400021' as the address, Peshawri lists 'Lunch & Dinner' without times, and several hotel and restaurant listings rely on hours notices rather than dated 2026 content. Haji Ali Dargah gives a location description rather than a postal address.
