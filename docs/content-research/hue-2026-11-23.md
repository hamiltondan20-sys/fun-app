## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and ticket details do not guarantee availability. The Hue Monuments Conservation Centre site (Imperial City, royal tombs, Royal Antiquities Museum) refused connection and its e-ticket portal showed no readable content, so the city's main heritage sights are absent; the Ho Chi Minh Museum site returned nothing readable, Bach Ma National Park had a certificate error, and Meliá Vinpearl Hue, Huong Giang Hotel, Highlands Coffee and Starbucks returned 403. Many Hue cafés and bakeries publish only on social media, which this review does not accept, so coffee and bakery coverage is limited to hotel lounges. Hotel Saigon Morin's hours come from listings dated September 2020, An Hien Garden House's hours and ticket price from a page updated January 2024, and Silk Path's dining hours from an undated page; Madam Thu, Phú Mộng, Le Soleil and Vạn Xuân show only a 2026 copyright as current evidence. Ancient Hue's restaurants and Phu Cam Cathedral publish no opening hours. The confirmed total (18) is below the 25–35 target because few Hue venues have readable official websites.
