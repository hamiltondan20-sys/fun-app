## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several confirmed places show only a 2026 copyright footer, menu file or booking policy as evidence of operation (Marble, Saint, Mix Cocktail Bar, Obscura, Bean There, Sakhumzi, Liliesleaf, Ditsong Military History, Everard Read), so they are rated medium. Hours were not published on the pages read for Mandela House, Everard Read, Bean There and Sakhumzi (buffet times only), and Gold Reef City Theme Park gives no daily hours; its address is taken from Tsogo Sun's Gold Reef City complex page. Johannesburg Zoo's visit page gives an 8:30 am opening while its fee page gives 8:00 am. Sites that could not be read: Four Seasons The Westcliff and Nelson Mandela Square (403), Victoria Yards events (403), Father Coffee and DW Eleven-13 (404), The Marabi Club (500), Kitchener's and Orlando Towers (connection refused), Sin + Tax, Doubleshot and Home of the Bean (DNS failure), Les Créatifs (SSL error). No food market could be confirmed. A first automated read of the Apartheid Museum visitor-information page reported text addressed to an AI, but a second read found none and no wording could be quoted, so nothing is listed under injectionLikeText.
