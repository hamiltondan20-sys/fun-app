## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several sites could not be read: the Hôtel Negresco and Café du Cycliste returned HTTP 429, the Hyatt and Radisson pages returned 403, La Merenda and Flaveur had SSL errors, and Le Safari, Old Sport Club and Hôtel Beau Rivage did not connect. Some confirmed places publish only partial details: Jan gives no exact days or times, Le Patio at Hôtel La Pérouse publishes no hours, and Chez Pipo's footer and map link give different postcodes (06300 and 06000). The Cours Saleya market has no city page that could be found, and MAMAC remains closed for renovation. The guide has no dedicated specialty coffee shop or bread bakery, because none could be confirmed on an official site.
