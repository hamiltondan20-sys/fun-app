## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several confirmed places published only partial information: the Catedral, Capilla Real and Basílica de San Juan de Dios showed only special or holiday hours, Monasterio de la Cartuja, Hammam Al Ándalus and Fundación Rodríguez-Acosta published no weekly hours, and Café Futbol, Malamiga, Bohemia Jazz Café, El Huerto de Juan Ranas and Cueva de la Rocío showed no 2026-dated content beyond published hours or bookings. The Museo Cuevas del Sacromonte gives its location only as Barranco de los Negros, and its www host had a certificate error, so the apex domain was read. The Centro Federico García Lorca homepage carries an unrelated gambling-app link, which suggests third-party spam on the site. Sites that could not be read include the Museo de Bellas Artes (Junta de Andalucía), Parador de Granada, Hotel Alhambra Palace, Casa Ysla, La Fábula, Puesto 43, Ruta del Azafrán and the Ayuntamiento de Granada monuments pages. The Generalife gardens exhibition at the Palacio de Carlos V ended on 24 May 2026.
