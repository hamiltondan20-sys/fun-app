## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several well-known Galway venues could not be read: The King's Head, Coffeewerk + Press, Sheridans Cheesemongers, McCambridge's, Rúibín, Monroe's, The Dough Bros and Hazel Mountain Chocolate returned rate-limit errors; Griffin's Bakery, Dela, Tartare and the Town Hall Theatre refused automated reading; The Crane Bar, Cupán Tae and Geometry Coffee failed on certificate errors; none of these failures is evidence of closure. Public areas such as the Latin Quarter and Salthill Promenade have no operator page and were not checked. Hours were not published for the Hall of the Red Earl, Sophie's at The Dean, Quincentennial Park and the University of Galway, Corrib Princess sailing times were not on its home page, Galway Cathedral's hours are described as approximate, and Gourmet Tart Co.'s Abbeygate Street Sunday hours appear to contain a typo. Galway City Council posted storm reopening notices on 25 and 27 October 2026 without naming the locations, so check outdoor sites on the day. Several confirmations (Ard Bia, Corrib Princess, Tigh Neachtain, O'Connor's Bar, Cava Bodega) rest on published hours or booking tools rather than dated 2026 content.
