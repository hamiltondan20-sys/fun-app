## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability, and hours may change for Ramadan and the Aid holidays. The Jardin Majorelle's site published hours but no street address on the pages that loaded, and its contact and French pages failed (HTTP 429/500), so it is not included. Several sites could not be read because of certificate or connection errors (Nomad, Le Jardin, L'Mida, Dar Moha, Pepe Nero, Musée de Marrakech, Kechmara, Four Seasons). Weekly hours were not published for La Mamounia, Royal Mansour, Al Fassia Aguedal, Le Trou au Mur, La Maison Arabe or MACAAL, and Pâtisserie Amandine did not state closing days. The end date of the Enki Bilal exhibition at Dar El Bacha was not published.
