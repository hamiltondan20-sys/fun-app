## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Only 22 places could be confirmed: many Gyeongju restaurants and cafés publish only on maps or social media, so food listings lean on hotel outlets at Kolon Hotel Gyeongju and Kensington Resort Gyeongju, and no cocktail bar could be confirmed (Moonlight Pocha is a chicken and draft-beer bar). Bulguksa Temple's hours come from an undated page; Seokguram Grotto's hours page returned an error; Gyeongju City pages give no hours for Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond, Daereungwon, Cheomseongdae, Wolseong or Gyochon, and their operating evidence is limited to current admission and 2026 route or event pages. Hours were not published for Olon Bakery Cafe or the Kensington outlets. Hilton Gyeongju (HTTP 403), Gyeongju Bird Park (certificate error), Lahan Select Gyeongju, Gyeongju World, Silla Millennium Park, Hanwha Resort Gyeongju, Yosokkoong, The-K Hotel Gyeongju and Gyeongju Arts Center could not be read.
