# Paris source review: draft

October 14, 2026. Research only: live guide data and publication flags are unchanged.
The evidence is in `paris-2026-10-14.json` in this directory. Owner approval is required
before this selection is applied and the guide's rendered content changes.

## Method

Every place named in the current Paris guide was checked, plus a few first-timer
candidates that aren't in it yet (Sainte-Chapelle, the Arc de Triomphe, Musée Rodin,
Palais Garnier, Musée de l'Orangerie, Galerie Vivienne, Sacré-Cœur, Notre-Dame, the
Centre Pompidou). Each was checked against its **operator's own website**, or the public body that runs it (Centre des monuments nationaux, the French
Senate, the City of Paris). Maps, review sites, blogs and news were not used. Sites
that refused automated fetches were read in a browser. A fetch failure is never treated
as a closure. One site showed a bot-check page; it was not bypassed.

## Reader copy the guide must carry

- **Louvre:** a timed reservation is required for all visitors from 17 October to
  1 November 2026. It is closed on Tuesdays.
- **Musée d'Orsay, Musée Rodin and Cité des sciences:** closed on Mondays (Cité des
  sciences opens on Mondays 19 October to 1 November).
- **Arc de Triomphe:** the lift is out of service until further notice, so the terrace
  is 284 steps.
- **Palais Garnier:** tickets are sold online only, and rehearsals can close the
  auditorium without notice.
- **Jardin du Luxembourg:** gate times change twice a month with daylight.
- **Le Jules Verne:** jacket required for men.
- **Septime:** tasting menus only; bookings open 21 days ahead.
- **Clamato:** walk-in only.
- **Bistrot Paul Bert:** phone bookings only; closed Sunday and Monday.
- **Du Pain et des Idées:** closed at weekends.
- **Poilâne:** closed on Sundays.
- **Holybelly:** closed 1 and 11 November and 21–27 December.
- **Branches:** the exact branch is named for every multi-branch business.
- **No promises:** no prices, Michelin ratings or availability are promised.
