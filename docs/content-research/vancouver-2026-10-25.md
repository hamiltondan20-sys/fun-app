## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Stanley Park (City of Vancouver), the Vancouver Aquarium and Dr. Sun Yat-Sen Classical Chinese Garden returned HTTP 403; Bill Reid Gallery, 49th Parallel, Revolver, Anh and Chi and Elysian Coffee returned HTTP 429; Science World, Vancouver Lookout, FlyOver Canada and Matchstick Coffee returned no readable content, so none of these are included, and none of these failures indicates a closure. Several confirmed places publish no dated 2026 content and rest on standing hours or booking notices (Joe Fortes, Miku, Café Medina, St. Lawrence, Botanist, The Pourhouse, Purebread, Beaucoup Bakery, Small Victory, Nemesis Coffee, Lonsdale Quay Market); Grouse Mountain, Café Medina and Published on Main publish no hours on the pages read; The Pourhouse shows two conflicting hour sets; and Beaucoup Bakery's Kitsilano hours are ambiguous in the page layout. The Diamond's former domain now redirects to another business. One automated summary of the Lonsdale Quay page mentioned AI-directed text, but a re-read found none, so nothing is quoted.
