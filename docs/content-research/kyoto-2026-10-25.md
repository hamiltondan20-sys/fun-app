## Limitations

This is a website review, not a visit or a phone check. Published opening, booking and exhibition details don't guarantee availability. Several official sites couldn't be read: Kyoto Imperial Palace (maintenance notice), Sanjusangen-do and Ryoan-ji (certificate or SSL errors), Heian Jingu and Shinshindo (connection refused), Hyotei, Kagizen Yoshifusa and Smart Coffee (502 errors), Four Seasons and Park Hyatt Kyoto (403), % Arabica (418), Kyoto Railway Museum and Shimogamo Shrine (no readable content), and Kyoto Aquarium's access page (timed out). Kinkaku-ji's only dated 2026 item is a September 28 cashless-payment notice. Hours weren't published or readable for Fushimi Inari Taisha, Nishiki Market, Toji, the National Museum of Modern Art, Weekenders Coffee, Stumptown Coffee, Mr. Maurice's Rooftop Bar and The Garden Bar. Yasaka Shrine lists only its office hours. Sizuya and Ippodo rest on company-wide 2026 notices, and Le Petit Mec's address is an English rendering from its shop list.
