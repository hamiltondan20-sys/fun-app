## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and show details do not guarantee availability. Hours for Raya, Graph Phuket and Crème Café come from undated pages, and no hours were published for Andamanda Phuket, Laguna Park, Mom Tri's Kitchen, Ta Khai, Talung Thai, Reflections Rooftop Bar or the Kata Rocks venues. Siam Niramit's schedule labels were partly unclear in the fetched text, so only the Tuesday closure and 80-minute show length are used. Several sites could not be read: Phuket FantaSea (403), Café del Mar Phuket (522), Aquaria Phuket (certificate error), Carnival Magic and Bartels (no readable content), The Boathouse and Blue Tree Phuket (connection failures), plus several domains that no longer resolve. Big Buddha, Campus Coffee Roasters and One Chun publish only on social media, the Phuket Elephant Sanctuary gives no exact address, and no independent bakery with a readable official site was found, so the bakery list relies on KR Hangout's pastries. A first read of flyinghanuman.com reported text addressed to an AI, but a second read found none, so it is not listed as injection-like text.
