## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Louvre Abu Dhabi, Zayed National Museum, Natural History Museum Abu Dhabi, Four Seasons, Conrad Etihad Towers, Shangri-La, Café Bateel and the World Trade Center Souk returned HTTP 403; Manarat Al Saadiyat and Brioche Dorée had certificate errors; % Arabica returned 418; teamLab Phenomena, Abrahamic Family House, Meylas and Emirates Park Zoo returned empty pages; Wahat Al Karama refused the connection. No market or food hall could be confirmed. Ferrari World, Warner Bros. World, SeaWorld and Yas Waterworld appear to be operating but publish no exact address or hours, so they were left out. The Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque's two official hours pages disagree on closing times; Erth and Jubail Mangrove Park show conflicting hours on their own pages; Ladurée (Abu Dhabi Mall) gives both Level 1 and ground floor. Hours for Cartel Coffee Roasters, Coya Abu Dhabi and Qasr Al Watan are not published, and hours for Tashas, 99 Sushi Bar, Buddha-Bar Beach, Ladurée (Yas Mall), the Cultural Foundation and the Falcon Hospital (page last updated 2018) come from undated or older pages. The bakery picks are patisserie-cafés; no bakery-only operator site could be read.
