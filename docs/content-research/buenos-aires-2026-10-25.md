## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several sites could not be read: Museo Nacional de Bellas Artes, Ecoparque, Palacio Libertad and Four Seasons (403), Planetario, Biblioteca Nacional and Catedral Metropolitana (certificate errors), Las Violetas (connection refused), Ninina (rate-limited) and Casa Rosada (timeout). No current official city page for Recoleta Cemetery was found, so it is omitted. Hours were not published for Florería Atlántico, Faena, the Alvear Palace venues, Atelier Fuerza, Museo de Arte Moderno and Temaikèn, and Gran Bar Danzón gives days but no times. Don Julio, La Cabrera, Gran Bar Danzón, Florería Atlántico and Café de los Angelitos showed no 2026-dated content, so they rest on published hours or booking channels. Cuervo Café publishes one chain-wide schedule rather than branch hours.
