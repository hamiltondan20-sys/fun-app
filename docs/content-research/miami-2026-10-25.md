## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Panther Coffee and Vice City Bean returned rate-limit errors, Oleta River State Park's state page refused automated reading (403), B Bistro + Bakery's domain did not resolve, Eternity Coffee Roasters had an SSL failure, Rosetta Bakery's store locator did not load, and The ScapeGoat's site gave no address, so none of these were used. All Day shows a temporary closure. Rubell Museum and Zoo Miami hours, and Greenstreet Cafe's closing time, were not published on the pages read; Boia De's hours lack AM/PM; several restaurant sites (Joe's Stone Crab, Boia De, Mandolin, Versailles, Café Demetrio, Threefold Cafe, Sweet Liberty) showed hours and booking tools but no dated 2026 content. Venetian Pool and the Coral Gables cafés are in neighbouring Coral Gables.
