## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several official sites could not be read: Hokkaido Shrine (expired certificate), Ramen Sumire (certificate mismatch), Kinotoya (403), Okurayama Ski Jump Stadium and JR Tower Observatory T38 (503), and Sapporo Park Hotel (DNS failure); Sapporo TV Tower's address could not be read. Opening hours were not published on the pages read for Odori Park, Sapporo Beer Museum, Nijo Market, Ebisoba Ichigen Sohonten, Soup Curry GARAKU and Ganso Sapporo Ramen Yokocho, and closing days were not shown for Sapporo Jogai Market or Miyakoshiya Coffee Honten. Nijo Market and Sapporo Jogai Market addresses are the cooperatives' published addresses. The Hokkaido University Botanic Garden season dates sit in a calendar image that could not be read, and Mikuni Sapporo's specific closure dates beyond Tuesdays were not readable.
