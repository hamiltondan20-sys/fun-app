## Limitations

This is a website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several official sites could not be read by automated fetch and were left out rather than bypassed: Top of the Rock, the American Museum of Natural History, MoMA and Grand Central Terminal (HTTP 403), The Met and the Brooklyn Museum (HTTP 429), the Guggenheim (cookie wall only), the NYPL main branch (empty page), Bubby's (connection refused), Mah-Ze-Dahr (HTTP 404) and NYC Parks' Central Park page (HTTP 403; the Conservancy site was used instead). Some places were only partly published. Crown Shy, Attaboy and Devoción showed hours but no dated content. The Whitney listed exhibitions without dates. The Frick page carried one apparently stale notice. Employees Only's reservations page may be out of date. Katz's listed weekly hours that conflict with its "open 24 hrs on weekends" banner. The High Line's closure notice and Central Park's event dates had no year shown.
