## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. The National Trust's Bath Assembly Rooms and Prior Park pages showed a bot check, Victoria Art Gallery's visit and contact pages refused automated reading (so its address could not be confirmed), and Colonna Coffee, Picnic Coffee, Comins Tea House, The Raven and GAIL's returned rate-limit errors; none of these failures is evidence of closure. Several confirmed places publish hours only partially or not at all (No.1 Royal Crescent, The Circus Restaurant, The Royal Crescent Hotel & Spa, The Gainsborough Bath Spa, The Bath Priory, Clayton's Kitchen, Green Park Station), Landrace and Noya's Kitchen show conflicting restaurant hours, and Bath Abbey's address is its published postal address. No standalone coffee shop could be confirmed, so the coffee list uses cafés and tea rooms.
