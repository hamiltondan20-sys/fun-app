## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. The Basilique Notre-Dame de Fourvière was left out only because its official site publishes no street address; its site was active and showed its gardens closed to 30 October 2026 for tower works. Sites that could not be read: Café des Fédérations (expired certificate), Le Bouchon des Filles (connection reset), Pralus (rate-limited), Cour des Loges (403), and Food Traboule (domain redirects elsewhere). Partially published: Institut Lumière, Villa Florentine, Fourvière Hôtel and Brasserie Georges show no hours on the pages read; Les Halles publishes stall hours rather than market hours; Le Sucre's hours vary by event. Several restaurants (La Mère Brazier, Takao Takano, Paul Bocuse, Bernachon, Slake) were confirmed by live booking or current hours rather than dated 2026 content.
