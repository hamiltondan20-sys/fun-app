## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several operator sites could not be read: St-Viateur Bagel, Schwartz's, Olive et Gourmando, Joe Beef, Café Olimpico, Hof Kelsten and Fous Desserts returned HTTP 429, the Musée d'art contemporain returned 403, Larrys had an expired certificate and Boulangerie Guillaume failed DNS lookup. Espace pour la vie pages (Botanical Garden, Biodôme) redirect to a calendar with no address. Hours were not published on the pages read for Notre-Dame Basilica, Château Ramezay, the Montreal Science Centre and Fairmount Bagel, and several restaurants, cafés and bars show only hours or booking links without dated 2026 text, so they are marked medium confidence.
