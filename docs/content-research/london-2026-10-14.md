# London source review: draft

October 14, 2026. Research only: live guide data and publication flags are unchanged.
The evidence is in `london-2026-10-14.json` in this directory. Owner approval is required
before this selection is applied and the guide's rendered content changes.

## Method

Every place named in the current London guide was checked. Six first-timer essentials
that aren't in it yet were checked too: the Tower of London, Westminster Abbey, the
National Gallery, Tate Modern, St Paul's Cathedral and the V&A. Each was checked
against its **operator's own website**: Historic Royal Palaces, the City of London
Corporation and the venues themselves. Maps, review sites, listings, blogs and news
were not used. Sites that refused automated fetches were read in a browser. Two sites
showed bot-check pages; they were not bypassed. A fetch failure is never treated as a
closure.

## Reader copy the guide must carry

- **Science Museum:** closed all day on 11 November 2026; free tickets must be
  pre-booked.
- **Sky Garden:** needs a free timed ticket (released each Monday, three weeks ahead).
  Capacity is reduced while a lift is out of service, and there are dated hire and
  maintenance closures.
- **Westminster Abbey and St Paul's:** no sightseeing on Sundays. Both can close at short
  notice for services.
- **National Gallery:** several rooms are closed for building work.
- **Tate Modern and St Paul's:** no large bags.
- **Borough Market:** closed on Mondays until 30 November.
- **Walk-in places:** Padella (virtual queue), the Connaught Bar, and Dishoom in the
  evenings for groups under six.
- **Rules:** children under 10 are not admitted after 7 PM.
- **Claridge's:** Festive Afternoon Tea from 3 November.
- **Eric's Bakery:** open Thursday to Saturday only.
- **The Breakfast Club:** closes at 3 PM on weekdays.
- **Branches:** the exact branch is named for every multi-branch business.
- **No promises:** no prices, ratings or availability are promised.
