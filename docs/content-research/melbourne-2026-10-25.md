## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several official sites could not be read: Melbourne Museum (HTTP 403), and South Melbourne Market, MoVida and most specialty-coffee roasters' sites (Market Lane, Seven Seeds, Patricia, Proud Mary, Industry Beans, Dukes, ST. ALi, Axil, Code Black) returned HTTP 429, so the coffee list is thinner than intended. Arts Centre Melbourne returned no readable content. Melbourne Zoo, the Shrine of Remembrance, the MCG, SEA LIFE Melbourne, Prahran Market and Gimlet were left out because no street address appeared on the pages read. Some confirmed places are only partly published: Melbourne Skydeck, Vue de monde and Brunetti Classico (Carlton) showed no hours; Queen Victoria Market and Collingwood Children's Farm give no street number; Supernormal, Cumulus Inc., Black Pearl, Siglo and Vue de monde showed no dated 2026 content beyond current hours or booking links. On the Queen Victoria Market getting-here page and the Baker Bleu locations page, the fetch summariser reported text addressed to AI assistants but did not return its wording, so it could not be quoted and may have been a misreading of the request.
