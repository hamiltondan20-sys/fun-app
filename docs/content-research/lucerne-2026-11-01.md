## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. KKL Luzern, Titlis and Max Chocolatier's store pages refused automated reading (HTTP 429), the old Historisches Museum site failed with an SSL error, and Seebad Luzern returned no readable content, so none of them are included. Chapel Bridge and the Lion Monument are left out because no current official page with an address was found. Some places are only partly published: Pilatus and Rigi gave no daily operating hours on the pages read, HEINI (Falkenplatz) hours are in a PDF that was not read, and the hours for Café Tacuba, Mill'Feuille, Old Swiss House and Wirtshaus Galliker appear on pages with no 2026 date. The Glacier Garden and Swiss Museum of Transport pages contain conflicting seasonal hours or end dates. Château Gütsch and Hotel des Balances give a street name but no house number, and the Schweizerhof Bar gives only "Schweizerhofquai".
