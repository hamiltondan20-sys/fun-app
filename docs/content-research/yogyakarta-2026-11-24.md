## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. InJourney's Borobudur, Prambanan and Ratu Boko ticket pages carry a 2025 footer and give start times but not closing times for Prambanan and Ratu Boko; their locations come from the operator's own map pins rather than street addresses. Museum Sonobudoyo's hours are labelled January 2026 and read 'Tuesday & Sunday', so check the full weekly schedule. ViaVia Jogja's page gives two different sets of hours. Hours were not published for Taman Pintar (shown only as an image), Gudeg Yu Djum Pusat, Eboni Bar & Lounge, Amanjiwo, Plataran Borobudur or The Phoenix Hotel's Vino Bar. Gembira Loka Zoo's latest dated news is from November 2025. Museum Affandi was left out because its October 2026 revitalisation notice could not be read. Sites that could not be read: borobudurpark.com, Museum Gunungapi Merapi, Melia Purosani and Hyatt Regency (HTTP 403), HeHa Sky View (suspended), Sumberwatu (no readable content). Many well-known warungs and cafés, including Kopi Klotok, Bale Raos and Mediterranea, have no official website and were not used.
