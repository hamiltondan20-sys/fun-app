## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability, and Japanese-language pages were read through automated extraction. Several sites could not be read: REC COFFEE branch pages returned HTTP 429, Grand Hyatt Fukuoka returned 403, Trandor had a certificate error, and Hakata Hanamidori, DACOMECCA and Ishimura Manseido did not resolve; IPPUDO, ShinShin and Nokonoshima Island Park returned nothing readable. The Kyushu National Museum was dropped only because no page read showed its street address, and Kushida Shrine because no official site was found. Hours were not published on the pages read for Ohori Park, Fukuoka Castle Ruins, Uminonakamichi Seaside Park and Yanagibashi Rengo Market (whose only dated item is from January 2026), and Marine World's closing days were not readable; the site also flags a temporary suspension of its dolphin and sea lion shows. manucoffee and Hakata Machiya Folk Museum hours come from undated shop-list and access pages, and Hotel Okura's Pastry Boutique page gives two different closing times (20:00 and 18:00).
