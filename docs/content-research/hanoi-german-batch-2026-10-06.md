## Known-wrong content audit and detection methods

Audit date: 2026-10-06. Exact-name searches across data/ and the generated
Berlin and Vienna pages found neither Cafe Central nor House of Small Wonder
nor the former Johannisstrasse address. Both public guide pages returned HTTP
200; their downloaded HTML also contained none of those names/addresses:
https://hamiltondan20-sys.github.io/fun-app/destinations/vienna/ and
https://hamiltondan20-sys.github.io/fun-app/destinations/berlin/.
No production removal was necessary. This is not a full verification of either
guide and does not change their undocumented status.
An additional accent-normalized scan of all JS/HTML under data/, destinations/
and countries/ also returned zero matches. The live checks were limited to
Berlin and Vienna, not every public page.

- Cafe Central: the venue's own homepage, https://cafecentral.wien/en/,
  surfaced the closure through its guest notice and reservation notice.
  It states closure from March 16, 2026 for renovation and a planned winter
  2026 reopening. Re-read on the audit date. The contact address still appears
  on the same page; checking only the address, name, or historical tourism
  coverage would not establish that the cafe currently welcomes visitors.
  DECENTRAL is advertised separately, not silently substituted for the cafe.
- House of Small Wonder: its own directions page,
  https://www.houseofsmallwonder.de/find-us, surfaced the stale-branch problem.
  It advertises brunch at Auguststrasse 11-13, 10117 Berlin, states that
  Johannisstrasse 20 remains closed, and warns that a ride-app name selection
  can route visitors to the former location. Re-read on the audit date.
  A name-only existence check would pass because the business still operates.
  The operator's explicit ride-app warning shows why a map/name lookup alone
  is insufficient. We did not independently test a ride app, and do not claim
  every tourism board or map service currently carries the stale address.

For future checks, match the exact branch and full address against current
operator directions, notices, and reservation scope. A reachable website or
a familiar proper noun is not operating evidence for a particular branch.

## Publication checklist

1. Finish pending operator reads and independent corroboration where available.
2. Record exact recommendation name, branch, checked URLs, date, operating
   evidence and original `sourcedFor` in the per-recommendation ledger.
3. Replace unsupported recommendations with empty fields, never generic text.
4. Audit changed-page counts before generating. Stop on reductions or unrelated
   generated changes; do not weaken any gate.
5. Run the existing complete generation, release, publication-gate, link,
   JSON-LD and sitemap-date checks and inspect mobile/desktop output.
6. Commit only relevant files, synchronize git-derived sitemap dates, verify
   CI consistency, publish the tested batch and check public pages.
7. Report four-city counts and reuse statistics once complete. Then propose
   concrete category consolidation without implementing it.
