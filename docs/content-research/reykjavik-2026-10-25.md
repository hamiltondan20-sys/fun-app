## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several sites could not be read: Sky Lagoon (JavaScript loading screen only), Bæjarins Beztu Pylsur and Kolaportið (HTTP 429), Sægreifinn, Kaffi Vínyl and junglebar.is (domains did not resolve), and Hlemmur Mathöll (certificate error); none of these failures is evidence of closure. Grandi Mathöll, the Botanic Garden, Þingvellir, the Maritime Museum and Árbær Open Air Museum were left out because their pages showed no exact address or hours. Dill and the Settlement Exhibition did not publish readable hours; Reykjavík Art Museum showed no exhibition dates; Hallgrímskirkja's readable hours date from its May 2026 summer notice and may differ in winter. Sandholt, Mokka, Reykjavík Roasters, Whales of Iceland and Slippbarinn showed current hours but no 2026-dated content.
