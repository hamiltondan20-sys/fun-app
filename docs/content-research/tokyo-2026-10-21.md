## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several sites could not be read: SHIBUYA SKY and teamLab Planets returned only empty script shells, Bills returned no content, and Sushi Daiwa, Bar Benfiddich, Path and 365 Days failed DNS lookups. fuglen.com had an expired certificate, which was not bypassed. None of these failures is evidence of closure. Tsukiji Outer Market was left out only because its official pages give no postal address. Some places were published only partially: Ueno Zoo's visitor hours page did not load, Shinjuku Gyoen's hours and Narisawa's and The SG Club's hours were not published, and Narisawa's address appears only in a map link. Udon Shin, Glitch Coffee, AFURI, Fuglen Tokyo, Ivy Place and the Imperial Palace East Gardens are supported by currently published hours or booking links rather than 2026-dated content. Tokyo Skytree's stated hours were shown as 'today's' hours without a date. Fewer than 25 places were confirmed, because many input entries were generic or unreadable and the gap-candidate limit of 8 was reached.
