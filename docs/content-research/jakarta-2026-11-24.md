## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several Jakarta operators could not be read: the Kota Tua, Monas, Museum Sejarah Jakarta, Galeri Nasional, GBK, SKYE, Modernhaus, The Cocktail Club, Kaum and Tuku sites did not resolve; the National Library, ANRI, Four Seasons, Grand Hyatt, Starbucks and M Bloc sites returned 403; % Arabica returned 418; KidZania reset the connection; Jakarta Aquarium & Safari and the Hermitage returned server errors; and the Planetarium and Sate Khas Senayan refused connections. Ancol and Kempinski publish no street address. Hours are not published for Masjid Istiqlal (visitors), Taman Mini Indonesia Indah, Café Batavia, Patio Bakery, Tanamera Coffee or Bakoel Koffie, and the Tugu Kunstkring Paleis and Lara Djonggrang pages show ambiguous closing times. The Langham, Plataran, Café Batavia, Bakoel Koffie and Patio Bakery pages carry no dated 2026 content, so their hours and booking details are as published on undated live pages.
