## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several confirmed places (Palácio da Bolsa, Caves Cálem, Casa da Música) publish no readable weekly hours, and Serralves' hours headings showed no times; Café Santiago, Cantinho do Avillez, Manteigaria, Nata Lisboa, The Royal Cocktail Club and Museu Nacional Soares dos Reis showed current hours or booking but no dated 2026 content. Euskalduna Studio, Pedro Lemos and So Coffee Roasters had certificate errors; O Paparico and WOW returned 403; Combi Coffee and Fábrica Coffee Roasters returned 429; Do Norte Cafe, Negra Cafe, C'alma and 17º had no official site found; World of Discoveries and the Gaia cable car gave no street address. None of these failures is evidence of closure. Gaia venues are in Vila Nova de Gaia, across the river from Porto.
