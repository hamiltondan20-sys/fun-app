## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several major sights could not be read by automated fetch and are left out: the Terracotta Warriors museum site (failed fetch and an http-only redirect), the City Wall (SSL error), Hanyangling Museum (TLS error), Daming Palace and the Bell and Drum Tower (DNS failures), the Tang West Market Museum (connection reset) and the Xi'an Museum at the Small Wild Goose Pagoda (no readable content); Shangri-La, Grand Hyatt, Westin, Hotel Indigo and Hilton pages returned 403 and % Arabica returned 418. Hours for the Xi'an Banpo Museum, Huaqing Palace and the Ritz-Carlton venues come from undated pages; the Accor hotel venues other than Azur's breakfast publish no hours, and their operating evidence is live hotel booking rather than dated notices. The Xi'an Beilin Museum's pages disagree on closing time (18:00 vs 18:30) and on ticket price, so neither price is stated. Xi'an's independent restaurants, cafés and bakeries rarely publish official websites, so dining, coffee and bakery picks are hotel venues; the bakery list uses lounges that serve pastries, cakes or desserts rather than true bakeries.
