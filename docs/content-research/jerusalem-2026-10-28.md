## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability, and many venues change hours around Shabbat and Jewish holidays. Several major sites could not be read by automated fetch and are left out: the Israel Museum (certificate error), Tower of David Museum, Yad Vashem, the Biblical Zoo and the Ramparts Walk (HTTP 403), and the Knesset (blocked by location). Some places were published only partially: the Western Wall has no street address (the Prayer Plaza is the location named); the Begin Heritage Center's hours appear reversed on its page; the Museum on the Seam's English and Hebrew hours conflict; Bloomfield Science Museum, Neeman Bakery branches, the King David Hotel and the Jerusalem Cinematheque publish no weekly hours on the pages read; Caffit (German Colony) omits Thursday and Hasadna omits Sunday and Monday. Gethsemane, the Austrian Hospice, Café Hillel, Friends of Zion Museum and the Hebrew Music Museum showed no 2026-dated content, so they rest on published hours notices.
