## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Babu's Bakery, Vicafe, Max Chocolatier and MAME returned HTTP 429 errors; Kafi Schoffel had a certificate error; Bauschänzli, Rheinfelder Bierhalle, Café Schober and St. Jakob Beck domains did not resolve; Museum Rietberg and Uto Kulm pages were readable but lacked hours or operating evidence, so all of these were left out. Published only partially: Sprüngli (Paradeplatz) and The Dolder Grand did not show hours on the pages read, ZSG lake boats showed a timetable PDF but no hours on the page, the Botanical Garden showed no November-March hours, and Kronenhalle, Zeughauskeller, Haus Hiltl, Swiss Chuchi and Old Crow showed hours and booking details but no dated 2026 content. The Kronenhalle homepage also contained an unrelated promotional paragraph linking to a medication site, which may indicate the page has been tampered with.
