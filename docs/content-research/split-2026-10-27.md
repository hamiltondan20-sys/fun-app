## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Only 23 places could be confirmed, below the 25–35 target, because many Split venues publish only on social media or third-party listings, and several official sites could not be read: Bokeria and D16 Coffee (HTTP 522), Konoba Matejuška (certificate error), the Croatian National Theatre (connection refused), Le Méridien Grand Hotel Lav (HTTP 403), Hotel Atrium (connection refused), and the Cornaro Hotel and Hotel Peristil (title only). No official cathedral site was found. Hours are not published for the North Wall Walk, Ethnographic Museum, Marjan Forest Park, Kadena, Brasserie on 7, Pandora GreenBox, Hotel Park Split and Hotel Vestibul Palace. Konoba Varoš, Brasserie on 7 and Oš kolač show no dated 2026 content, and Marjan Forest Park is confirmed only through its operator's office address. The bakery and cocktail lists are thin: they rely on a pastry shop and on a hotel bar and a late-opening restaurant.
