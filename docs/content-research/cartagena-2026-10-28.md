## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability, and most sites read carry no dated 2026 hours notice, so hours are reported as each site lists them. The Castillo's hours come from an undated operator page that conflicts with an older 2023 notice (7 a.m. versus 8 a.m. opening), and Di Silvio's Getsemaní opening time differs between its branch page and FAQ. Celele, Alma, Erre, Townhouse and the Teatro Adolfo Mejía publish no opening hours. Sites that could not be read include the Museo del Oro Zenú (bot check), Restaurante Interno (expired certificate), Café Havana (DNS failure), Época and Libertario (rate-limited), Carmen and María (domains now redirect elsewhere) and the national park page for Corales del Rosario (404). No bakery could be confirmed: Pastelería Mila's only listed address is marked "coming soon", so the bakery list is empty.
