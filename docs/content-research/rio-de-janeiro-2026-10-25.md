## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several sites could not be read: Theatro Municipal and Garota de Ipanema (expired certificates), Museu Nacional de Belas Artes, Artesanos Bakery and Hotel Santa Teresa (DNS failures), Bar do Mineiro (connection reset) and Bar Lagoa (nothing readable); no official site was found for Padaria Nema. MAR and IMS Rio are temporarily closed per their own sites, and at Copacabana Palace, Cipriani and the Pool Bar are under renovation. Hours were not published on the pages read for Trem do Corcovado, Cristo Redentor, CCBB Rio, Parque Lage, Bar Urca, Aprazível, Churrascaria Palace, Confeitaria Colombo, Cafeína and the Copacabana Palace and Fairmont venues (except Tropìk). Several restaurants and cafés show no dated content beyond a 2026 copyright, booking links or menus. Election-day (25 October) notices at MAM Rio and CCBB were left out as short-lived. The MAR homepage contained unrelated gambling links that appear to be injected spam.
