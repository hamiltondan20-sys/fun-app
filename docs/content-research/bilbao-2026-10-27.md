## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several confirmed places (Zortziko, Etxanobe, Sorginzulo, Bar El Globo) publish no opening hours, and Café Iruña, Mina, Casa Rufo, Bascook, Gure Toki, La Mula de Moscú and Gin Fizz showed no 2026-dated content, so they rest on booking links or published hours (medium confidence). Puente Bizkaia's regular hours were not published, only a temporary lift notice, and its listed address is the operator's office in Las Arenas (Getxo). The Catedral de Santiago page shows two slightly different closing times. The Guggenheim's exhibition dates use an ambiguous date format and were read as month.day. Sites that could not be read: the Artxanda funicular (bilbao.eus, 403), Athletic Club museum (403), Radisson and Meliá (403), Gran Hotel Domine (expired certificate), Hotel Ercilla (certificate mismatch), Víctor Montes (SSL error) and Basílica de Begoña (connection refused). Euskal Museoa's domain redirected to an unrelated site, and Azkuna Zentroa's hours and programme were not readable. Pastelería California's morning hours were only partly readable, and Martina de Zuricalday publishes no hours or dated content.
