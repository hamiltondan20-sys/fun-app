## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Some places were published only partially: Orizuru Tower's hours appear only as an image; Peace Memorial Park, Café Jardin, Sky Lounge Top of Hiroshima, The Lounge, Taoli and Fruitier publish no readable hours; the Peace Memorial Hall shows only the day's hours; Mitchan Sohonten's October 28, 2026 closing-day change notice could not be read. Itsukushima Shrine (footer © 2015), Anagomeshi Ueno (footer © 2015) and Obscura Coffee Roasters show hours on undated pages with no 2026 content, so treat those hours as published, not verified current. Hiroshima Castle's main keep closed in March 2026, and the Hiroshima Museum of Art plans a renovation closure from August 1, 2027. Sites that could not be read: Okonomimura and Hiroshima Botanical Garden (connection refused), Fujiiya (HTTP 403), Iwaso and Otafuku Wood Egg (404), Hiroshima Carp (title only), and several assumed domains that did not resolve (Nagata-ya, Kakiya, Sheraton Grand Hiroshima, Hilton Hiroshima, Miyajima Brewery, Hiroshima City Transportation Museum). The Hiroshima City Museum of Contemporary Art and Mazda Museum were left out because no address and hours, or no street address, were readable.
