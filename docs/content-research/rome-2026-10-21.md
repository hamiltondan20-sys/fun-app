## Limitations

This is a website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. The Colosseum and Roman Forum could not be checked because colosseo.it refused automated connections and the ticketing site showed a bot check. Galleria Borghese's site also refused connection, and Forno Campo de' Fiori, Sant'Eustachio Il Caffè, The Court, Trapizzino, Pasticceria Regoli and Panella could not be read (connection refused, redirect loop, 403, 500 or maintenance page). Roscioli publishes no street address, and no official sites were found for Barnum Cafe, Marigold, Pergamino, the Spanish Steps or Villa Borghese. Hours are not published for La Pergola, Pizzarium or the Appia Antica park. St. Peter's Basilica gives no street address, and Da Enzo al 29 does not state its Sunday status. Jerry Thomas Speakeasy's site carries no 2026-dated content beyond live hours and booking. Only 18 places were confirmed, below the 25–35 target.
