## Limitations

Website review, not a visit or telephone confirmation. Advertised opening, booking and exhibition details do not guarantee availability. Several official sites could not be read: Museu CR7 (domain did not resolve), Museu de Arte Sacra do Funchal (HTTP 522), Pereira d'Oliveira (connection reset), and regional culture pages for Universo de Memórias, Casa-Museu Frederico de Freitas and Quinta Magnólia (HTTP 404). Some confirmed places are published only partially: Kampo, Akua and Armazém do Sal publish no opening hours; Blandy's Wine Lodge's tour pages give tour times but not general opening hours; the Jardim Botânico da Madeira page lists weekday hours without saying whether they cover the garden or the office; The Ritz Madeira gives no exact daily times; Teatro Municipal Baltazar Dias gives no box-office hours. Il Gallo d'Oro is listed by its operator as temporarily closed for renovations, and Museu Quinta das Cruzes has announced planned building works without stating any closure.
