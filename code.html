What does Horizon Bound actually do?

It turns a destination, dates, budget, and preferences into a vacation draft that feels personal, memorable, and easy to enjoy.

Can I still change the itinerary?

Yes. Users can swap ideas, remove stops, update logistics, compare alternate versions, and save the arrangement they like best.

What is the difference between preferences and non-negotiables?

Preferences shape the trip. Non-negotiables are the things the trip must respect, like accessibility needs, hard budget limits, or must-do events.

Will the app ever push too hard against what I want?

No. The planner can gently point out trade-offs or suggest a better-fit alternative, but the final choice still belongs to you.

Where do the guides live?

City and country inspiration live in Explore. Trip-specific guide context appears again inside Your Trip when it helps explain the plan.

Do I need to book through the app?

No. The planner can recommend flights and where to stay, then link out to book when you are ready.

Where is my beta trip data saved?

During beta, saved trips, profile fields, booking notes, and backups stay local to this browser. There is no online account sync yet, so export a backup before switching devices or clearing browser data.
